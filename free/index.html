Read the opening of The King in Yellow

A forbidden play ruins everyone who reads it to the end. Chambers never lets you see it; you only meet the people it has already claimed. Weird fiction has been borrowing from these stories since 1895: the cursed play, the lost city of Carcosa, the Yellow Sign.

Your sample is the opening of the restored, annotated Carrier Press edition: the introduction and two complete stories, "The Repairer of Reputations" and "The Mask," with three of its illustrations. It arrives in your inbox as EPUB and PDF, yours to keep.
