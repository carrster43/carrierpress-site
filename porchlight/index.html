Porchlight Halloween Map

Community listings for organizers

If you are running a trunk-or-treat, a school Halloween night, a fire station open house or a church parking lot event, a community listing puts it on the map that local families are already holding in their hands on the night.

Where this stands right now. Porchlight Halloween Map has been built and submitted to Apple, and is waiting on App Store review. Listings are being reserved now and go on the map the day it goes live. Nothing is being charged for this season, so reserving one costs you a few minutes and risks nothing at all.

Why a listing reaches further than anything else on the map

Porchlight shows a family two different things. Houses handing out candy are searched tight, about 1,500 meters, because that is a walk with a four year old in a costume. Community events are searched wide, 25,000 meters, about fifteen miles, because a family will get in the car for a trunk-or-treat.

That difference is deliberate and it is written into the app. The widest reach in Porchlight belongs to community events. Nothing a household can do puts a pin in front of as many families as your listing does.

What a listing actually is

A pin on the map and a card in the list, carrying what you wrote:

  • Who is running it. Saint Anne's Parish. Ladder 12. Oakmont PTA.
  • What it is called, and up to 500 characters of detail in your own words.
  • Where. The address, plus a line of plain direction if the address alone will not do it, such as "back lot, enter from Third Street".
  • When it starts and ends. Events are ordered on the map by start time.
  • Allergy safe and step free, if they apply to you. Families filter on both. If your event has a non-candy table or a ramped entrance, say so, because that is the reason a particular family drives to you.

What a listing is not

This is not advertising, and the distinction is structural rather than a promise about our intentions.

  • There is no targeting. Your listing is shown to every family in range, and we do not know anything about them to target with.
  • There is no auction and no bidding. Paying more does not move you up. Events are ordered by start time, the same as everything else.
  • There is no impression tracking, no click tracking and no third-party advertising code anywhere in the app, so we cannot report how many people saw your pin. We would rather tell you that than invent a number.
  • Your listing is not marked as paid to the families who see it. A paid listing is content you wrote, sitting on the map beside everything else.

Porchlight is free for households, permanently. Charging the families who make the map is how you end up with an empty one, so organizers are the only people who will ever be asked for money, and this season they are not being asked either.

What it costs

Nothing, this year.

Founding season. One event, Halloween 2026. No account, no subscription, no card, nothing that renews.

Community listings are the one thing Porchlight will ever charge for, and they are free for Halloween 2026 because this is the map's first night and nobody has seen it yet. We cannot show you how many families your pin reached last year, because there was no last year. Charging for reach we cannot evidence is not a thing we are willing to do.

So the founding season is free, and it is free for the reason that should make you trust it rather than suspect it: an empty map helps nobody, least of all the families it was built for. The organizers who fill it this year are doing us the favor.

Listings will cost money in 2027. Anyone listed this year will be told what that price is before it applies to them, and will never be enrolled in anything automatically.

Reserve a listing

How to reserve one

Email support@carrierpress.com with the details above and we will confirm and place it. A person reads every one, because a person has to put the pin on the right spot, and an event in the wrong parking lot is worse than no event at all.

If it is easier, send it as plain prose rather than a form. Anything that answers who, what, where and when is enough to start.

The dates that matter

  • Reserve by 17 October. That leaves time to confirm the location and get the listing on the map while families are still deciding where to go.
  • Halloween falls on Saturday 31 October 2026.
  • Every pin is deleted the night it ends. Yours included. Porchlight does not keep a record of who ran what or which doors expected children, so there is no archive sitting on a server on the first of November.

Questions

What is the catch on a free listing?

There is not one. No card is taken, no account is created, nothing renews, and your details are not sold or passed to anyone. The only thing we get out of it is a map with events on it.

Do families need an account to see my event?

No. Signing in is only needed to add your own porch.

Can I change the details after I send them?

Yes, up to the night. Email the same address.

Our school or parish needs paperwork on file for this.

Say so in the email and you will get written confirmation of the listing at no charge, or a W-9, whichever your office needs. Nothing is being billed, but a zero dollar record is easy to produce and some offices require one.

Our event is not really a trunk-or-treat.

Fine. Haunted walks, church festivals, fire station open houses and library nights all belong on the map. If children are going somewhere on Halloween, it is the kind of thing a family is looking for.
