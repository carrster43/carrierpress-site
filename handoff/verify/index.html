Check a Handoff record

If someone has handed you a record exported from Handoff, you can check here that no message in it was changed, removed or reordered after it was written. The check runs in your own browser: nothing you paste or open is sent anywhere, and you do not need an account or a key.

What the check shows

  • Each message carries a hash of its own contents and of the message before it. If any message after it was edited, deleted or moved, the hashes stop matching from that point, and this page says where.
  • It shows the messages were not altered after they were recorded. It says nothing about whether what they say is true.
  • Times are the Carrier Press server's clock, not an independent timestamping authority.
  • A record cut short at the end still checks out on its own. That is why every export states the total number of messages and the final chain hash: compare them with the other parent's copy.

How it is checked

For each message, a SHA-256 hash of its number, author reference, author name, time and text, then a SHA-256 hash of the previous message's chain hash joined to that. It is the same calculation the Handoff app makes when a message is recorded, so a record that checks out here is one the app would also call consistent.

An export of part of the record lists the messages outside its dates by their number and two hashes only, without their text. Those keep the chain linked from the first message to the last, so a removed or reordered message still shows, but their wording is not checked here.
