Funding

Carrier Press is funded by its readers and listeners, and by nobody else. There are no investors, no grants, no publisher advances and no ads in any app. Every cost is paid from book sales and from people who choose to support the work directly.

What support pays for

  • Getting apps into the App StoreApple's developer membership and the build service that turns code into an app.
  • Getting books onto shelvesISBNs, printed proofs, and bookstore distribution fees for every format.
  • Getting books readAdvance reader copies, reviews outreach and audiobook editions.
  • TimeEvery month of support is time spent finishing the in progress list above instead of working around it.
