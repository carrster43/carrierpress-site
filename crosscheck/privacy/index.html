Crosscheck privacy policy

Last updated 21 November 2026.

Crosscheck compares a medical bill against the statement your insurer sent for the same care, and writes out the questions the difference raises. This policy explains what is kept, where it sits, and who can reach it.

This is health information, and it is treated that way

A bill and an explanation of benefits describe what was done to your body, by whom, and when. There is no useful way to soften that, so the rest of this page is specific rather than reassuring.

What is stored

  • Your email address, and a subscription status field that is unused in this version.
  • Each case: the figures you entered from the bill and from the insurer's statement, and the differences between them that Crosscheck worked out.
  • Each letter it composed for you, kept so you can find it again.

What is not stored

No password, because there is not one. Signing in is by a code sent to your email address. No insurance member number, no policy number, no card details and no clinical notes are asked for as fields.

Nothing is sent to a model, and nothing is sent to your insurer

Crosscheck does the comparison with ordinary arithmetic inside the app. No part of your case is sent to a language model or to any other company for processing.

It also never contacts anybody on your behalf. It writes a letter and hands it to you. Whether it is sent, to whom, and what happens next is entirely yours, and we have no visibility into any of it.

Who can see it

You, and nobody else using the app. Every table checks the signed in account against the owner of the row before it returns anything. There is no sharing feature, no advocate view and no employer or insurer access. We do not sell any of it and we do not send it to advertisers.

Who else is involved

Supabase hosts the database and sends the sign in codes. This app has no in-app purchase in this version, so nothing about a payment or a subscription reaches Apple or anyone else.

Deleting it

Deleting your account removes your profile, every case and every letter, immediately and unrecoverably. Save any letter you still need before you delete.

The web version

Crosscheck in a browser, at carrierpress.com/crosscheck/app, has no account and no database. Everything above about your email address, sign-in codes, Supabase and deleting an account is about the phone app. In the browser there is no sign-in at all.

Your cases stay in your browser. The figures you type from each bill and Explanation of Benefits (the amounts, the bill's lines and the service dates), the provider and insurer names, the account, claim and member numbers, the name for your letters, your own reason paragraph, each case's status and your outcome note are kept in that browser's storage for carrierpress.com (crosscheck.cases), on that device. Nothing you type is uploaded or stored on any server. The web version keeps no photographs.

Findings and letters are worked out in your browser, from those figures, each time you open a case. The letters are not kept as separate copies. Copy, print or download a letter and it goes to your clipboard, your printer or a text file on your device; Crosscheck sends nothing to your provider, your insurer or anyone else.

The one thing that is sent. When you open a case's letters with a license key, your browser sends that key and the case's random id, made up in your browser and saying nothing about you or the bill, to a small server of ours on Cloudflare. It asks Gumroad whether the key is valid and, for a Crosscheck key, asks Gumroad to count one case against it. Gumroad keeps that count. So that asking again for the same case never uses up a second one, our gate keeps the random case ids each key has opened, filed under a one-way scrambled form of the key, never the key itself; it keeps nothing else about you or your bill. No figure, name or letter is ever sent. Once the letters are open, the case keeps a note of it in your browser: the date, the last four characters of the key and whether it was a Crosscheck key or a Carrier Press Access key. The key itself is not kept.

No AI, no analytics. Crosscheck on the web makes no AI calls. Apart from loading its pages, the key check above is the only request it makes. Contact support opens your own email app, and nothing is sent unless you send it.

Your copy. Clearing the site's data in your browser erases your cases, so Account offers Download a copy (every case, as one file saved to your device). Restore from a copy reads a downloaded copy in your browser and adds the cases that are not already there; it never changes the figures in one that is, and the file is not sent anywhere.

To erase it, open Account, then Erase everything in this browser, which deletes every case and the figures in it. Deleting a single case removes it the same way. We cannot recover either, because we never had them. A key already used for a case's letters stays used, and erasing does not refund a purchase.

Payments are processed by Gumroad. Crosscheck never receives or stores your payment details. carrierpress.com is served through Cloudflare, which handles your IP address and each request the way any web host does.

On your phone

The app keeps your sign in on your phone, in the phone's secure keychain, so you stay signed in between launches. It also keeps the small rating note described below.

The rating prompt and Contact support

After something in the app has gone well, it may now and then ask Apple to show the App Store's own rating sheet. So that it asks rarely, it keeps one small note on your device: how many times things have gone well, the last two days they did, and the app version it last asked on. Nothing you entered is in that note, and it never leaves the device. Whatever you choose in the rating sheet is handled by Apple.

Contact support opens your own mail app with a message addressed to support@carrierpress.com. The subject line carries the app's name, its version and your phone's system version, so we know which build you mean. Nothing is sent unless you send it, and the app is not told whether you did.
