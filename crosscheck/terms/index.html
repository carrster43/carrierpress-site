What Crosscheck is, and what it is not

Crosscheck reads a hospital bill against the explanation of benefits, finds the lines that do not agree, and writes an appeal letter that cites the reasoning it used.

Crosscheck is not a lawyer and not a billing advocate, and nothing it produces is legal advice. It does not represent you, it does not file anything, it does not talk to your insurer or your provider, and it is not running a case on your behalf. The letter is a draft for you to read, change and send as your own.

It can be wrong about a line, and an appeal it supports can still fail. Appeals run on deadlines set by your plan and your state, and missing one is final: the app does not track them for you and cannot extend one. The app is held on regulatory grounds and is not for sale while that is unresolved.
