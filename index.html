The author

Jeffrey L. Carrier

Jeffrey L. Carrier writes fiction about the things families leave unsaid: inheritance, memory, and the quiet cost of a choice that looked simple from the outside.

His novels run from literary suspense (The Sponge Cache and the Greek diaspora Things Left Open series) to small town cozy mystery (The Marjorie Corey Files) to the literary Keystone Cycle. Whatever the shelf, the pull is the same: a documented record, a family secret, and someone who decides to keep reading when it would be easier to stop.

Before he wrote fiction he served in the U.S. Navy and the U.S. Air Force.

He lives on the Mississippi Gulf Coast with his two sons.
