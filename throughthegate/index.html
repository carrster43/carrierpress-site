Last updated 24 November 2026.

Through the Gate shows you the structured record a resume parser builds out of your resume: the fields it keeps, the wording it changes, and the parts of the document that do not become a field at all.

Through the Gate in your browser

Through the Gate is on sale now in a browser, from the Through the Gate page. The iPhone app is not out yet, so this is the version you can buy today. It is bought on Gumroad, not from Apple, in one of two ways: a monthly subscription, or a 90-day pass paid once that never renews (the prices are on the Through the Gate page).

  • Your key. The Gumroad receipt email carries a license key. Paste it on the Through the Gate page, under Already have a key?, in each browser you use. Keep that email: you need the key again in any other browser. A Carrier Press Access key on the Apps or All Access tier opens Through the Gate too, for as long as that membership lasts, and a Founding access key opens it for its first year.
  • The 90-day pass. It opens Through the Gate for 90 days from the day you buy it and then stops; there is nothing to cancel. After that, what you saved stays readable in that browser, parsing new resumes is paused, and a new browser will not open with that key.
  • There is no account. No password and no sign-in code. Your resumes, their readings and your applications are kept in that browser, on that device, and not on our server. Another browser or device cannot see them.
  • Keep a copy. Clearing this site's data in your browser, or a private window once it closes, erases everything, and Safari erases what a site saves on its own after about a week without a visit. We cannot bring it back, because we never had a copy. On Account, under Your copy, Download a copy now and then. Restore from a copy adds what is not already here and never replaces anything, which is also how you move to another browser.
  • How many. Up to 20 resumes can be parsed a day.
  • Reminders. A browser cannot remind you of anything when it is closed, and Through the Gate sends no email. The Applications tab shows what has had no reply when you open it.
  • Cancelling. The subscription renews every month until you cancel. Cancel in your Gumroad library, or write to support@carrierpress.com and we will cancel it for you. The period you paid for runs out first. After that your resumes and applications stay in the browser, and what pauses is parsing new ones.
  • Refunds. Apple has nothing to do with a web purchase, so write to support@carrierpress.com about one.
  • Leaving a shared computer. Lock Through the Gate in this browser, on Account, forgets your key here and keeps everything. Erase everything from this browser removes it for good. Neither one cancels your Gumroad purchase.

Why your job title was read as something you did not write

Screening software stores a title it can match against other titles, so an unusual one gets normalized on the way in. Where that happened, the record shows both: the wording you used, and the wording it was read as.

This is not an error to report. You are seeing the version of you that gets filed, which is the version nobody ever shows the applicant.

Why parts of my resume are under "What did not survive"

Content that a reader would see can still fail to become a field. Two column layouts, achievements set inside a graphic or a table, and loosely written dates all lose material on the way through extraction. Each item is listed with the reason it did not make it.

The flags above it are separate, and cover four things: a gap between positions, something a parser expected and did not find, something it could read more than one way, and a formatting problem. Where there is something worth changing, the flag says what.

Will this tell me whether I will get the job?

No, and it is built so that it cannot. Every employer runs a different stack, configured differently. What you see is how a competent general parser reads your document. It is not any one company's system, it does not know which company you applied to, and it does not predict whether you will be screened in or out.

Your resume is sent to Anthropic to be read

The parsing is done by a large language model run by Anthropic, not by code on our own machines. When you ask for a parse, the full text you pasted goes through a small server of ours to Anthropic's API and the result comes back to your browser; that server keeps neither. A resume is not anonymous: yours almost certainly carries your name, email, phone, where you live and everywhere you have worked, and all of it goes, because a parser that only saw part of it could not tell you what a real one would discard.

If you would rather that did not happen, the example on the Through the Gate page was run on an invented resume, and shows the kind of thing a parse reports.

The application tracker

On the Applications tab you can record a company and a role, dated the day you add them, and move each one through applied, screening, interview, offer, rejected, or no reply. Anything still marked applied after thirty days is flagged as having had no reply in a month. That is a count of days, not an opinion about the employer or the posting.

In the iPhone app

The iPhone app is not out yet. It will be sold as a subscription through your Apple ID, and its current price is on the purchase screen inside the app, which reads it from the App Store for your country.

A subscription bought through the App Store is managed on your device: Settings › your name › Subscriptions. Cancelling there stops the renewal and leaves the current period running to its end. We cannot cancel it for you. If you reinstall or change device, use Restore purchase. Apple is the merchant of record, so refunds are requested from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Your data

In a browser there is no account: your resumes, readings and applications stay in that browser, as above. There is no recruiter view, no employer access and no sharing feature in either version.

In the iPhone app there is no password; signing in is a code sent to your email address. Stored there: your email, how many parses you have run, every resume you saved as the complete text you pasted, every parse, and any applications you tracked. Deleting your account from the Account tab removes all of it, immediately and permanently, so copy out anything you want to keep first. The full policy is at carrierpress.com/throughthegate/privacy.
