What Through the Gate is, and what it is not

Through the Gate shows you the structured record a resume parser builds from your resume, and everything it threw away. One resume is parsed in full, free, so you see the whole record before you decide anything.

Your resume is sent to Anthropic to be read. The parsing is done by a large language model rather than by code on our machines, and a resume is not anonymous: yours almost certainly carries your name, address, employers and dates, and all of it goes. The privacy policy covers this in full, and the sample on our website runs on an invented resume if you would rather it did not happen.

It is one parser's reading, not the one an employer will use. Every applicant tracking system parses differently, and what this app discards is not necessarily what theirs will. A clean record here is not a prediction about any employer's software, is no indication of whether you will be contacted, and is not career advice. Priced by the month because a job search ends.
