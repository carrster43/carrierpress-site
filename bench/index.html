Bench support

Last updated 23 November 2026.

Bench is a set of AI desks, each run by someone who knows the work. One key opens two: the Author desk and the Errands desk. Bench drafts and plans; you send, file, book and pay.

The Author desk

Pick a job, fill in the form and the desk drafts it: a listing package (book description, subtitle check, seven KDP keywords, Amazon store categories for KDP and BISAC subjects for IngramSpark), front and back matter, back-cover and A+ copy, or a pitch email for a library or an independent bookstore.

Every draft is checked before you see it. A warning at the top names the words it found and why they matter: a best-seller or award claim your material does not make, an ISBN or a price you did not type, a placeholder such as [to be assigned] left in, praise that is not word for word from the review lines you gave, keywords that do not fit KDP's boxes, or a back cover over the word budget for your trim. A warning never hides or rewrites the draft: you decide.

The Errands desk

Write down the errand you have been putting off. The desk plans it in steps: what to find out and where to check it, options side by side, a message ready to send, form answers to paste, and, always last, the one thing you do yourself.

A drafted message can only be addressed to an address you typed in Addresses a draft may go to. Leave that empty and the plan has no drafted message.

What "Refused" means

If a plan contains a step that would act for you (send, book, pay, submit and so on), has no step left for you to do, or addresses a message to someone you did not name, Bench throws the whole plan away and shows you the reason, word for word. It never removes the bad step and gives you the rest, because a plan that tried to act is not one to half trust. Run it again, or reword the errand.

Why it never sends anything

There is no send button in Bench, and no way for it to post, file, book or pay. Open in my mail app opens a message in your own mail program, unsent. Ask the operator does the same, addressed to support@carrierpress.com with your draft in it.

Why it does not look things up

The desks do not browse the web. They work from what the model already knows and what you tell them, so a plan says where to check something rather than claiming it checked. A web address in a draft that you did not give is flagged. Check category paths, opening hours and prices against the source before you rely on them.

Your key, and runs

Enter the license key from your Gumroad receipt once on the Bench page, in each browser you use. A Carrier Press Access key on the Apps tier opens Bench too. Each desk allows 10 runs a day; the count starts again the next day. When a subscription ends, your past work stays readable and new runs pause.

Where your work is kept

In the browser you use, on that device, up to the last 200 drafts and plans. Not on our server. Another browser cannot see it, and clearing the site's data erases it, so use Download a copy on Your work now and then. Restore a copy adds a downloaded copy back.

Privacy

The full policy is at carrierpress.com/bench/privacy. What you type goes through our server to Anthropic to write the draft, and nothing is stored on our server. Drafts are written by an AI model: read every word before you use it.
