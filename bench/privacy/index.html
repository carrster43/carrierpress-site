Bench privacy policy

Last updated 22 November 2026.

Bench is a set of AI desks for particular trades, used in a browser at carrierpress.com/bench/app. It has no account, no sign in and no database. This policy explains what leaves your browser and what does not.

What you type is sent to Anthropic to write the draft

When you run a job, the desk and the job you chose and what you typed into its form go through a small server of ours on Cloudflare to Anthropic, which writes the draft. Whatever you put in the form goes, including any part of your book you paste. Our server stores none of it. Anthropic does not use data submitted through its API to train its models.

Your drafts stay in your browser

Each draft, with the form you filled in, is kept in that browser's storage for carrierpress.com, on that device, up to the last 200. It is not stored on any server of ours. Another browser or device cannot see it, and clearing the site's data in your browser erases it, so Your work offers Download a copy. Restore a copy reads a downloaded copy in your browser and adds what is not already there; the file is not sent anywhere. Erase everything removes every draft from that browser.

Bench sends nothing for you

Bench drafts. It never sends, posts, files or books anything on your behalf. Open in my mail app and Ask the operator open a message in your own mail program, unsent; nothing goes until you send it yourself.

Information about other people

A pitch email or an About the Author page can name people. Put in only what you would be comfortable sending to us and to Anthropic, because that is where it goes while the draft is written. A desk for a regulated trade refuses, in your browser and before anything is sent, text that looks like a health detail, a Medicare number, a Social Security number or a date of birth.

Your license key

Bench opens only with a license key, from your Gumroad receipt or a Carrier Press Access key. When you paste it on this app's page, it goes to our server on Cloudflare, which asks Gumroad whether the key is good and stores nothing. If it is, your browser keeps the key in an encrypted cookie that scripts on the page cannot read, and about once a day the server asks Gumroad again, so that access ends when a purchase is refunded or a membership ends. The cookie also counts the runs taken that day on each desk, so that one key cannot be used without limit. When access ends, your drafts stay readable and can still be downloaded, but new runs are paused. The server keeps no list of keys or visitors.

Payment

Gumroad handles the payment. Card details go to Gumroad and never to us.

On your phone

The app keeps your sign in on your phone, in the phone's secure keychain, so you stay signed in between launches. It also keeps the small rating note described below.

The rating prompt and Contact support

After something in the app has gone well, it may now and then ask Apple to show the App Store's own rating sheet. So that it asks rarely, it keeps one small note on your device: how many times things have gone well, the last two days they did, and the app version it last asked on. Nothing you entered is in that note, and it never leaves the device. Whatever you choose in the rating sheet is handled by Apple.

Contact support opens your own mail app with a message addressed to support@carrierpress.com. The subject line carries the app's name, its version and your phone's system version, so we know which build you mean. Nothing is sent unless you send it, and the app is not told whether you did.
