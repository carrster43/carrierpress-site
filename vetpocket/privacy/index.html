Vet Pocket privacy policy

Last updated 17 November 2026.

Vet Pocket keeps your pet's records and helps you judge whether something is an emergency visit or can wait until morning, with a cost range on each path. This policy explains what is kept.

What you describe is sent to Anthropic

This is the part you are least likely to assume, so it is here rather than at the bottom. When you ask whether to go now or wait, what you typed about your pet's symptoms is sent to a large language model run by Anthropic, and the assessment comes back.

Nothing is sent unless you ask for one. Recording a weight or a treatment stays on our database.

It is not a veterinarian, and it never is

On the privacy page because it bounds what the app is. Vet Pocket helps you decide whether to make a call. It does not diagnose, does not prescribe, and is not a substitute for a vet. If you think your animal is in danger, go, and do not wait for an app to agree with you.

What is stored

  • Your email address, and whether your subscription is active.
  • Each pet: the details you entered about them.
  • Weights and treatments, with their dates.
  • Each triage: what you described and what came back.

What is not stored

No password, because there is not one. Signing in is by a code sent to your email address. No veterinary practice records, no pet insurance details, no microchip number and no payment card details.

Who can see it

You, and nobody else using the app. Every table checks the signed in account against the owner of the row before returning anything. There is no vet view, and no practice or insurer has any route to it. We do not sell any of it and we do not send it to advertisers.

Who else is involved

  • Anthropic receives what you describe when you ask for a triage.
  • Supabase hosts the database and sends the sign in codes.
  • RevenueCat sits between the app and the app store and tracks whether your subscription is active. It receives the purchase events and an identifier for your account.
  • Apple handles the payment itself. Card details go to Apple and never to us.

The web version

Vet Pocket in a browser has no account and no database. Everything above about accounts, our database host, notifications on the phone, Apple and RevenueCat is about the phone app.

Your pets' records stay in your browser. Your pets, their weights, treatments and due dates, every triage, and the area you type in are kept in that browser's storage for carrierpress.com, on that device. They are not sent to us and not stored on any server. Another browser or device cannot see them, and clearing the site's data in your browser erases them, so Account offers Download a copy. Restore from a copy reads a downloaded copy in your browser and adds what is not already there; it never replaces anything, and the file is not sent anywhere.

A triage. When you ask, the animal's species, breed, age, sex and neutered status if recorded, its recorded conditions and medication, its last three weights, what you typed, how long, and the area you typed are sent, through a small server of ours on Cloudflare, to Anthropic to be read, and the answer comes back to your browser. Neither we nor that server keep any of it.

Reminders. The web version sends no reminders. When you choose Add every due date to my calendar, your browser makes a calendar file with each pet's name, the treatment and its due date, and saves it to your device for you to open in your own calendar. It is not sent to us.

Your license key. The web version opens only with a license key, from your Gumroad receipt or a Carrier Press Access key. When you paste it on this app's page, it goes to that same server, which asks Gumroad whether the key is good and stores nothing. If it is, your browser keeps the key in an encrypted cookie that scripts on the page cannot read, and about once a day the server asks Gumroad again, so that access ends when a purchase is refunded or a membership ends. The cookie also counts the questions asked that day, so that one key cannot be used without limit. The server keeps no list of keys or visitors. Remove my key from this browser, on the app's page, deletes it.

Payments are processed by Gumroad. Vet Pocket never receives or stores your payment details. carrierpress.com is served through Cloudflare, which handles your IP address and each request the way any web host does.

To delete it, open Account, then Erase everything from this browser. Nothing about your pets is held anywhere else to delete. Lock Vet Pocket in this browser forgets your key here and keeps everything for when you enter it again.

Deleting it

Deleting your account removes your profile and every pet, weight, treatment and triage, immediately and unrecoverably.

On your phone

The app keeps your sign in on your phone, in the phone's secure keychain, so you stay signed in between launches. It also keeps the small rating note described below.

The rating prompt and Contact support

After something in the app has gone well, it may now and then ask Apple to show the App Store's own rating sheet. So that it asks rarely, it keeps one small note on your device: how many times things have gone well, the last two days they did, and the app version it last asked on. Nothing you entered is in that note, and it never leaves the device. Whatever you choose in the rating sheet is handled by Apple.

Contact support opens your own mail app with a message addressed to support@carrierpress.com. The subject line carries the app's name, its version and your phone's system version, so we know which build you mean. Nothing is sent unless you send it, and the app is not told whether you did.
