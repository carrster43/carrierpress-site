Every comparison carries these lines, word for word, where your client reads them:

  • How plans are rankedPlans are ranked only on what you told us: your medicines, your doctors, and how you weigh cost against coverage. No insurer pays to appear higher, and your agency cannot change the order.
  • What the estimate isThis is an estimate built from the plan's published costs and the medicines you listed. It is not a quote and it is not a guarantee. Formularies and networks change, and only your agent can confirm what a plan will actually cost you.
  • What the tool coversThis tool compares the plans loaded by your agency. It may not include every plan available in your area. It is not connected to Medicare or to any government agency, and it does not enrol you in anything.
  • The hand-offNothing here enrols you. Take this comparison to your agent, who is licensed to check it against your prescriptions and doctors and complete an application with you.

Built inside the Medicare marketing rules

Plan Finder is a comparison tool. It is built so that the three things that would turn a tool into marketing are not there to switch on:

  • No pay per lead or per enrollmentYour agency pays a flat licence. Nothing is paid per lead, per enrollment or as a share of commission, and there is no place in the system to record such a term.
  • No steeringThe ranking takes no carrier preference and no agency setting. The order comes only from what the client said.
  • No enrollmentThe comparison ends with a hand-off to your licensed agent. There is no way to submit an application through it.

Your agency's own marketing obligations, including the CMS disclaimer where your agency is a third party marketing organization, stay with your agency and its compliance team. We are glad to walk your compliance reviewer through how it works before you sign.

Carrier Labs LLC does not sell, offer, market or represent any Medicare plan, is not paid for leads or enrollments, and is not connected with or endorsed by Medicare, CMS or any government agency. Plan Finder does not enroll anyone in a plan. Questions

Is Plan Finder sold to people on Medicare?

No. It is licensed to agencies and used by their licensed agents with a client. Nothing on it is sold to a beneficiary.

Can we favour the carriers we contract with?

No. There is no setting for it, by design. The order comes only from the client's own medicines, doctors and priorities, and every comparison tells the client so.

Where does the plan data come from?

From the plan files CMS publishes each year, loaded for the counties you serve. The comparison tells the client it covers the plans your agency loaded, which may not be every plan in the area.

Does it check provider networks?

It shows how many of the client's doctors a plan lists, and always tells the client to confirm with the practice, because provider directories are often wrong.

How is it priced?

Per agency, by the number of agent seats. Tell us your team size in the form and we send a written quote.
