Carrier Labs

Built to do one job. Ready in your browser today.

Alongside the books, Carrier Labs makes small apps, each built for one kind of person and one job. A symptom log that shows your doctor what tends to happen together. The file your family will need if something happens. A homework helper that asks the next question instead of handing over the answer. Rain for the game table.

They share a design position. No streaks, no gamification, no engagement loop, no advertising, and no analytics. A tool you use less often because it worked is a tool that worked.

Cast: every character, in their own voice.

Cast performs a book the way a full cast would: a narrator, and a separate voice for each character, kept the same every time you open the book so you learn who is who. The Carrier Press shelf is ready to hear in your browser today, and Cast will perform EPUB files of your own too.
