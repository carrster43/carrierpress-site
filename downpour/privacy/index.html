Downpour privacy policy

Last updated 24 September 2026.

Downpour plays rain for sleep. The rain is built live on your phone rather than played from a recording, so nothing is streamed or downloaded while you listen and it works with no signal at all.

There is no account and no server

You do not sign in, because there is nothing to sign in to. This app has no database of ours, no cloud sync and no backend.

Nothing is stored

Downpour does not save anything about you, even on your own phone. The surface, treatments, volume and sleep timer you choose are held only while the app is open and are forgotten when it closes.

It asks for no permissions: not your microphone, not your location, not your contacts and not your photos.

Who else is involved

Nobody. Downpour sells nothing inside the app, so there is no payment and no payment details. There is no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising and no tracking software of any kind, and no server of ours for anything to be sent to.

Deleting it

Delete the app. There is no account to close and no data held anywhere else, by us or by anyone, so there is nothing to ask us to remove.
