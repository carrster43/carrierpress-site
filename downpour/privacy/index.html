Downpour privacy policy

Last updated 4 November 2026.

Downpour makes rain for tabletop game masters, in your browser, at carrierpress.com/downpour/app. Every drop is computed in the browser as it falls, so no sound is streamed or downloaded while you listen.

There is no account and no server of ours

You do not sign in, because there is nothing to sign in to. Downpour has no database of ours and no cloud sync. The pages are plain files served by GitHub Pages, which, like any web host, sees the address your browser connects from when it sends them.

What stays in your browser

These sit in your browser's own storage for this site, and only there:

  • Whether Downpour is unlocked here, and whether a membership is the reason.
  • The settings you last used: the rain you shaped, the Night Arc, the volume and the fade timer, so it opens the way you left it.
  • The rains you save: the name you gave each one and how it is shaped.
  • A Carrier Press Access key, only if you unlocked with one, with the dates Gumroad last answered about it. A membership can end, so the page checks this key again about once a day while you use it. Every Carrier Press web app on this site reads the same entry, because one membership opens them all.

A one-time Downpour key is not kept. It is checked once, and the browser remembers only that it is unlocked.

None of it is uploaded to us. The one thing that ever leaves is a license key, on its way to Gumroad. Your saved rains do not follow your key to another browser.

Downpour asks for no permissions: not your microphone, your camera, your location or your notifications. It copies text to your clipboard only when you press Copy, and it asks the browser to keep the screen awake while the rain plays.

Who else is involved

  • Gumroad sells the unlock and takes the payment, so we never see your card. We receive only what Gumroad tells any seller about a sale. When you paste a license key, the page sends that key and the product's ID to Gumroad to check it, and nothing else: not your rains and not your settings.
  • MET Norway answers the weather question, and only when you open Rain Window. The page asks about the cameras' locations, up to twelve at a look, never yours. The request comes from your browser, so MET Norway sees its address, as any website would.
  • YouTube shows each Rain Window camera in its own player, from youtube-nocookie.com, muted. Nothing from YouTube loads until you open Rain Window. Once it does, Google handles that player under its own policy, and it sees your browser's address like any video you watch. Nothing about the camera you looked at comes back to us.

There is no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising and no tracking software of ours in Downpour, on the free listen or after you buy.

Writing to us

Write to the maker opens your own mail app with a message addressed to support@carrierpress.com. The subject line carries Downpour's name and version. Nothing is sent unless you send it, and the page is not told whether you did. If you do, we have your email and whatever you wrote, and use them to answer you.

Deleting it

Delete a saved rain with its own Delete button. To remove everything, clear this site's data in your browser's settings. That also clears what the other Carrier Press web apps keep here, because they share carrierpress.com. Downpour then opens fresh, with no rains and no unlock, and pasting your key from the Gumroad receipt brings the unlock back without paying again. There is no account to close and nothing of yours on a server of ours to remove. Gumroad keeps its own record of your purchase.
