Last updated 24 September 2026.

Downpour plays rain for sleep. The rain is not a recording: it is built live on your iPhone, drop by drop, for as long as it plays, so it never repeats and there is no loop to notice in the night.

Choosing a surface

Most of what you hear in rain is what it lands on, so Downpour offers five surfaces, each modelled separately.

  • Window: rain on glass heard from a dry room. Bright, glassy ticks over a thin sheet.
  • Tent: a taut nylon fly over an enclosed space. Each drop is a damped thud with real low end.
  • Tin roof: corrugated metal. The brightest of the five, and the only one that rings.
  • Car roof: a sealed cabin. The most low end, with a thin tick of glass on top.
  • Umbrella: the same nylon as the tent with the opposite sound. Almost no body, as if you were standing outside.

You can change surface while the rain is playing. The new rain takes over without a click or a gap.

Slower and Lower

Slower is lighter rain easing off: fewer drops, and the gusts drift more slowly. It is a different rain, not the same one played at a slower speed.

Lower takes the brightness off the top and brings the body up, so nothing sharp is left to catch your attention. Of the two, it is the better one for sleep.

Use either, both or neither. Five surfaces and four combinations make twenty different rains.

The sleep timer

Choose 15 minutes, 30 minutes, 1 hour or 2 hours, or Off to play until you stop it. When the time is up the rain fades out gradually instead of stopping dead, so the ending does not wake you. You can change the timer while the rain is playing and it starts counting again from that moment.

Playing with the screen locked

Downpour is built to keep playing with the screen locked and with the ring and silent switch set to silent. It also plays over AirPlay and Bluetooth speakers. If the rain stops when your phone locks, please tell us which iPhone and iOS version you have, using the address below.

No signal needed

Nothing is streamed or downloaded while you listen, so Downpour works in airplane mode, in a tent, or anywhere else without a connection.

Your data

There is no account, no sign in and no server. Downpour asks for no permissions and stores nothing about you: your surface, treatments, volume and timer are forgotten when the app closes, and it opens on the Tent each time.
