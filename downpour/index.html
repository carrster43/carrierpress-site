Downpour support

Last updated 5 November 2026.

Downpour makes rain for tabletop game masters. It runs in your browser at carrierpress.com/downpour/app. The rain is not a recording: every drop is built live, from what it falls on, for as long as it plays, so there is no loop for the table to notice. It is web only; there is no app to install.

Before you buy

The app opens only with a license key. The Downpour page has a 25 second clip of the Tent, made by the same code the app runs, and screens from the app. No account and no email address are needed.

Paying, and what you are charged

The unlock is one payment, not a subscription, so nothing renews and there is nothing to cancel. Gumroad sells it and takes the payment. The current price is on the Downpour page and on the unlock sheet inside the app, before you go anywhere near a checkout. It opens all nine surfaces, up to three layers at once, the ready scenes, Night Arcs, saved rains and codes, the fade timer, rain that plays until you stop it, and Rain Window without a time limit.

Your license key

  • The key is on the Gumroad receipt email.
  • On the Downpour page, paste the whole key under Already have a key?
  • On another browser or another computer, paste the same key there too. There is no account, so the key is how a browser knows. Downpour does not count how many browsers use it.
  • If the browser ever forgets (some clear a site's saved data after a stretch away, and clearing it yourself does too), paste the key again. You do not pay again.

If a key reads as not matching, copy it again from the receipt and paste all of it. If it still fails, or you cannot find the receipt, write to us with the email address you used at Gumroad.

A refunded or disputed purchase no longer unlocks Downpour when its key is checked.

Carrier Press Access

A Carrier Press Access key works in the same field, on the Apps or All Access tier. The Books tier covers the books only and does not open Downpour. Because a membership can end, the page asks Gumroad about that key again about once a day while you use it. If it cannot reach Gumroad, the key keeps working for seven days from the last answer. When a membership ends, Downpour in that browser goes back to the Tent alone, and your saved rains are still there.

Coming back to your rain

Downpour opens the way you left it: the rain you shaped, the Night Arc, the volume and the fade timer. The top of the screen says As you left it until you play or change something. Your saved rains are one press away under the scenes, and in My rains further down.

All of that lives in the browser you made it in. It does not follow your key. To move a rain, or to keep one safe, press Copy beside it: you get a short code to paste into My rains anywhere, or into Discord for another table. A code carries the rain. A Night Arc is named in the copied text and set by hand.

At the table

It plays while the browser tab stays open. Phones pause browser sound when the screen locks, so a laptop or tablet by the table works best. Pick a scene, or shape your own, and change it mid-session: the new rain crossfades in, never a cut. Scene view is a full-screen moving picture of the rain, drawn live, to share in Discord or capture in OBS. It is rain only: no music, no thunder and no tavern voices, so it sits under whatever else you play.

Rain Window

Rain Window checks the weather at a list of live public cameras, finds one where it is raining now and daylight if it can, and shows it full screen with your rain as the sound. If nowhere it asked is raining, it says so and shows the place with the most rain due. Each camera is its owner's own stream, in YouTube's player, muted, with the owner's name on screen.

It needs a connection. A camera can go dark when its owner stops the stream; the list is checked and dead ones are taken out, but one can stop between checks. After you unlock, press W for another. Without an unlock, reload the page to look somewhere else.

If the rain will not start

Browsers only allow sound after you press something, so nothing plays until you press Play. Check that the tab is not muted and the volume in Downpour is up. It needs a browser with Web Audio worklets, which current Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari all have. If it still says the rain could not start, tell us which browser and the small line under the message.

A problem with a purchase

Write to us and say what went wrong. Gumroad took the payment, so any money going back would go back through Gumroad.

Your data

There is no account. Your settings and rains stay in your browser. A license key goes to our key check and to Gumroad to be checked, and Rain Window talks to MET Norway and YouTube. The privacy page has all of it.
