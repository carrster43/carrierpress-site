Fluent Hour privacy policy

Last updated 19 November 2026.

Fluent Hour gives you one spoken scenario a day, about ten minutes, with no lessons and no streaks. This policy explains what is kept, where it sits, and who can reach it.

Your side of the conversation is sent to Anthropic

This is the part you are least likely to assume, so it is here rather than at the bottom. The other side of the conversation is a large language model run by Anthropic. What you say is transcribed to text and that text is sent so a reply can come back. There is no way to have the conversation without this, because it is the conversation.

What is stored

  • Your email address, the language you are learning, and whether your subscription is active.
  • Each session: the scenario, when it happened and how long it ran.
  • Each turn in it, as text: what you said and what came back.

Audio is not kept

Speech is turned into text so the conversation can happen, and the recording is not retained afterwards. What remains is the transcript. There is no voiceprint, no speaker model and no audio file of you on our servers, and nothing about your voice is used to train anything.

What is not stored

No password, because there is not one. Signing in is by a code sent to your email address. No name, no age, no location and no payment details.

Who can see it

You, and nobody else using the app. Every table checks the signed in account against the owner of the row before returning anything. There is no teacher view, no class and no leaderboard. We do not sell any of it and we do not send it to advertisers.

Who else is involved

  • Anthropic receives your transcribed turns, as above.
  • Supabase hosts the database and sends the sign in codes.
  • RevenueCat sits between the app and the app store and tracks whether your subscription is active. It receives the purchase events and an identifier for your account.
  • Apple handles the payment itself. Card details go to Apple and never to us.

Deleting it

Deleting your account removes your profile and every session and turn, immediately and unrecoverably.

The web version

Fluent Hour in a browser has no account and no database. Everything above about your account, Supabase, RevenueCat, Apple, stored sessions and turns and deleting an account is about the phone app.

Your conversations stay in your browser. The language and level you chose, every conversation with what you said, the replies and the corrections, and the minutes you spoke are kept in that browser's storage for carrierpress.com, on that device. They are not sent to us and not stored on any server. Another browser or device cannot see them, and clearing the site's data in your browser erases them, so Account offers Download a copy. Restore from a copy reads a downloaded copy in your browser and adds what is not already there; it never replaces anything, and the file is not sent anywhere.

What is sent for each turn. The words you said (as text, never audio), the situation, your language and level, and the conversation so far (up to the last forty turns) go through a small server of ours on Cloudflare to Anthropic, which writes the reply and any correction, and the reply comes back to your browser. No name, email address or account is sent, because there is none. Anthropic processes what it is sent to return the reply and does not train on it.

Speech recognition is your browser's own. Fluent Hour asks your browser to turn what you say into words. Some browsers send the audio to their maker to do that: Chrome sends it to Google, and Safari to Apple, under their terms rather than ours. We never receive audio. Where your browser cannot recognise speech, or you do not allow the microphone, you type instead. The replies are read aloud by your browser's own voice, so nothing is sent anywhere to be spoken.

Your license key. The web version opens only with a license key, from your Gumroad receipt or a Carrier Press Access key. When you paste it on this app's page, it goes to that same server, which asks Gumroad whether the key is good and stores nothing. If it is, your browser keeps the key in an encrypted cookie that scripts on the page cannot read, and about once a day the server asks Gumroad again, so that access ends when a purchase is refunded or a membership ends. The cookie also counts the turns taken that day, so that one key cannot be used without limit. The server keeps no list of keys or visitors.

Payments are processed by Gumroad. Fluent Hour never receives or stores your payment details. carrierpress.com is served through Cloudflare, which handles your IP address and each request the way any web host does.

To delete it, open Account, then Erase Fluent Hour from this browser. Nothing about your conversations is held anywhere else to delete. Lock Fluent Hour in this browser only forgets the key.

On your phone

The app keeps your sign in on your phone, in the phone's secure keychain, so you stay signed in between launches. It also keeps the small rating note described below.

The rating prompt and Contact support

After something in the app has gone well, it may now and then ask Apple to show the App Store's own rating sheet. So that it asks rarely, it keeps one small note on your device: how many times things have gone well, the last two days they did, and the app version it last asked on. Nothing you entered is in that note, and it never leaves the device. Whatever you choose in the rating sheet is handled by Apple.

Contact support opens your own mail app with a message addressed to support@carrierpress.com. The subject line carries the app's name, its version and your phone's system version, so we know which build you mean. Nothing is sent unless you send it, and the app is not told whether you did.
