Quiet support

Last updated 3 October 2026.

Quiet is a personal record that never leaves the device it was written on: notes, voice memos and photographs, kept in a searchable archive with no account, no sync and no server behind it.

There is nothing to sign into

There is no sign-in screen, no password and no email address, because there is no account. Everything you keep is stored inside the app's own storage and none of it is uploaded. The app contains no code that sends your journal anywhere, and there is no server of ours for it to go to. That is a property of how it is built rather than a promise about how we behave.

What that costs you, and what to do about it

There is no backup on our side and no second device: we cannot restore your journal, because we never had it. Your iPhone's own backup, to iCloud or to a computer, includes Quiet the way it includes every app, and restoring it brings your journal back with its photos and memos. If the phone has no backup and is lost, or you delete the app, the journal is gone.

Export everything, in Settings, makes one zip file: your words as plain text plus every photo and voice memo. Export words only makes just the text. A single photo or memo can be shared from its entry.

If other people use your phone, Settings can require Face ID or your passcode to open Quiet. It is off until you turn it on, and it can never lock you out: your phone's passcode always opens it.

Why the search tells you what it understood

Quiet reads a search as three things, a time, a kind of thing and some words, then says so in a line under the box: "Looking through photos, from last summer, mentioning mum."

That line is the point. When nothing comes back, read it first: usually the question was misread rather than the memory being missing. Quoting a phrase makes it literal.

It handles today, yesterday, this week, last month and last year, "three months ago" as a window rather than one day, bare years, month names, and seasons.

"Last summer" searched the wrong months

Seasons depend on which half of the world you are in, so it is a setting rather than an assumption. Settings, then Where you are, states which months "last summer" currently means for you.

Voice memos are not searched by their words

A voice memo is recorded, stored and played back, and it is found by whatever text you typed alongside it rather than by what you said. If you will want to find one later, write a line with it.

Photographs are copied in

A photograph you pick is copied into Quiet's own storage rather than pointed at in your camera roll, so deleting the original later does not empty a page of the journal.

Writing about another day

The date row at the top of the writing page files an entry under the day it is about, so a note written today about last Tuesday is kept, and searched, as last Tuesday. To move an entry you already kept, open it and tap Change day.

The unlock

Quiet holds your first ten entries so you can try it. Searching, exporting and deleting keep working after that, so nothing you wrote is ever held back. Unlocking removes the limit for good: one payment, no subscription, nothing to renew and nothing to cancel. The price on the unlock button comes from the App Store for your own country rather than from this page. The unlock is remembered on the device, so the app works with no signal at all.

Restoring a purchase

Settings, then Restore a previous purchase. It asks the App Store what your Apple account already owns, and it is there whether or not this device believes it is unlocked. If a restore finds nothing, check you are signed in with the Apple ID that bought it, which is the cause almost every time.

Refunds

Apple is the merchant of record, so refunds are requested from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com. We cannot issue one.

Deleting

Settings, then Delete everything. It removes the entries and the photograph and recording files themselves, immediately: a delete that leaves the files on disk is not a delete. Export first if there is anything you want, because there is nothing on our side to restore from.

What the app asks for

  • The microphone, when you press record.
  • Your photo library, when you pick a photograph to copy in.

Nothing else. No location, no contacts, no calendar, no notifications, no analytics and no advertising identifier. The full policy is at carrierpress.com/quiet/privacy, and it is short: the only outside party involved at all is Apple, which handles the purchase and tells us only that one was made.
