FirstDay privacy policy

Last updated 18 November 2026.

FirstDay turns the school's emails and PDFs into one calendar, one supply list and one form tracker. This policy explains what is kept, where it sits, and who can reach it.

What you forward is sent to Anthropic to be read

This is the part you are least likely to assume, so it is here rather than at the bottom. When you forward an email or upload a PDF, its full contents are sent to a large language model run by Anthropic, which reads it and returns the dates, the supplies and the forms. The whole document goes, including anything the school put in it that you were not thinking about.

This is information about a child

School correspondence names your child, their class, their teacher and often their school. You are entering it, and it is held as your record as their parent.

We ask for as little about them as the app can work with: a name to tell one child's list from another's, and a year or class where a document mentions it. There is no date of birth field, no photograph, no address and no school login. Your child has no account here, we will never contact them, and nothing about them is used for anything but building your own lists.

What is stored

  • Your email address, and whether your subscription is active.
  • Each child you set up, by the name you gave.
  • Documents you forward or upload, and the structured records read out of them.
  • Deadlines, supply items and form statuses, with the dates they are due.
  • A record of reminders sent, so the same one is not sent twice.

What is not stored

No password, because there is not one. Signing in is by a code sent to your email address. No school portal credentials, no child's date of birth, no photographs, no grades and no payment details.

Who can see it

You, and nobody else using the app. Every table checks the signed in account against the owner of the row before returning anything. There is no school view, no teacher access and no sharing between parents. We do not sell any of it, we do not send it to advertisers, and nothing about a child is used for advertising in any form.

Who else is involved

  • Anthropic receives the documents you forward, as above.
  • Supabase hosts the database and sends the sign in codes.
  • Resend delivers reminder emails, and receives your email address and the contents of that reminder.
  • RevenueCat sits between the app and the app store and tracks whether your subscription is active. It receives the purchase events and an identifier for your account.
  • Apple handles the payment itself. Card details go to Apple and never to us.

The web version

FirstDay in a browser has no account and no database. Everything above about accounts, sign in codes, our database host, reminder emails, Apple and RevenueCat is about the phone app.

Your children's names and your letters' dates stay in your browser. The children you add, what was read out of each letter (its title, its dates, forms, amounts and supply items, and anything that could not be filed), and your warning and reminder settings are kept in that browser's storage for carrierpress.com, on that device. They are not sent to us and not stored on any server. Another browser or device cannot see them, and clearing the site's data in your browser erases them, so Account offers Download a copy. Restore from a copy reads a downloaded copy in your browser and adds what is not already there; it never replaces anything, and the file is not sent anywhere.

Reading a letter. When you choose Read it, the text you pasted, or the photo or PDF you chose, is sent through a small server of ours on Cloudflare to Anthropic to be read, and what was read comes back to your browser. The names you added in FirstDay are not sent with it (the letter may name your child itself): your browser matches the names afterwards. The letter itself is not kept, in your browser or anywhere else: neither we nor that server store it.

Reminders. The web version sends no reminders and no email. When you choose Add to my calendar or Add every date to my calendar, your browser makes a calendar file with each confirmed date, its title, the child's name if one was matched, and the title of the letter it came from, and saves it to your device for you to open in your own calendar. It is not sent to us.

Your license key. The web version opens only with a license key, from your Gumroad receipt or a Carrier Press Access key. When you paste it on this app's page, it goes to that same server, which asks Gumroad whether the key is good and stores nothing. If it is, your browser keeps the key in an encrypted cookie that scripts on the page cannot read, and about once a day the server asks Gumroad again, so that access ends when a purchase is refunded, a membership ends or a school-year pass runs out. The cookie also counts the letters read that day, so that one key cannot be used without limit. The server keeps no list of keys or visitors. Remove my key from this browser, on the app's page, deletes it.

Payments are processed by Gumroad. FirstDay never receives or stores your payment details. carrierpress.com is served through Cloudflare, which handles your IP address and each request the way any web host does.

To delete it, open Account, then Erase FirstDay from this browser. Nothing about your children is held anywhere else to delete. Lock FirstDay in this browser forgets your key here and keeps everything for when you enter it again.

Deleting it

Deleting your account removes your profile, every child you set up, every document and everything read out of them, immediately and unrecoverably.

On your phone

The app keeps your sign in on your phone, in the phone's secure keychain, so you stay signed in between launches. It also keeps the small rating note described below.

The rating prompt and Contact support

After something in the app has gone well, it may now and then ask Apple to show the App Store's own rating sheet. So that it asks rarely, it keeps one small note on your device: how many times things have gone well, the last two days they did, and the app version it last asked on. Nothing you entered is in that note, and it never leaves the device. Whatever you choose in the rating sheet is handled by Apple.

Contact support opens your own mail app with a message addressed to support@carrierpress.com. The subject line carries the app's name, its version and your phone's system version, so we know which build you mean. Nothing is sent unless you send it, and the app is not told whether you did.
