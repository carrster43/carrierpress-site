Spellbright support

Last updated 22 November 2026.

Spellbright is a word game for children aged 4 to 12, played in a browser at carrierpress.com/spellbright/app. Every game is free to play. A short ad shows now and then between games, and a membership turns the ads off.

Getting started

The first screen asks how old the player is: 4 to 12, or Grown-up. The age sets the words and the board, and it can be changed from the home screen at any time. The game gives each player a made-up name such as Purple Comet 42, so a child never types one. There is no account, no email address and no download.

The games

  • Adventure: a map of five worlds, each with its own robot, and the robots get stronger world by world.
  • The tile board: play a robot, or two to four people taking turns on one device.
  • Crossword and Letter Crossword, which starts with some letters already in place.
  • What's the Word?: read a meaning and pick the word.
  • Word Ladder, Word Search, Mystery Word and Scramble.
  • Silly Sentences: a family card game for two to six people on one device, or with robots.

When people of different ages play together, the youngest player's age sets the words everyone plays with. Ages 4 and 5 collect stars instead of points.

Help is always free

Every game has a Help button. It never costs anything and can be pressed as often as a child likes. On the tile board it lights up the letters that can make a word and lists every word that fits; in the puzzles it gives a nudge, such as the next letter of a crossword word.

This week's spelling words

A grown-up can type or paste the list from school, behind a short grown-up question. The child then practises the words the way school teaches it: look, cover, spell, check. Two right in a row and a word is mastered. From the home screen, the same words can be played in Crossword, What's the Word?, Word Search and Mystery Word.

Reading aloud

Words and their meanings are read aloud in a natural recorded voice. Anything without a recording is read by the best voice your browser has. Ages 4 and 5 hear every clue; older players tap to hear one. If you hear nothing, check that the tab is not muted and the volume is up.

The ads

An ad shows only at a natural break, after a finished puzzle, round or game. Never during a turn, never in front of Help, never in spelling practice and never on a grown-ups screen. None shows in the first two minutes, and never two within three minutes. Every ad request is marked for a child and asks for ads that are not personalised. If an ad does not load, the game simply carries on.

Turning the ads off

The membership is on Spellbright's page. It turns the ads off and changes nothing else. Your license key is on the Gumroad receipt email: enter it once on that page in each browser your family uses. A Carrier Press Access key on the Apps or All Access tier works too. Cancel in your Gumroad library; when the membership ends, the game keeps working, with the ads back.

Where things are kept

The age, the spelling list and the adventure map stay in the browser you play in. They do not follow you to another browser or device, and clearing the site's data in your browser erases them. The privacy policy has the full list.
