Boat Ready: free reference

Required boat safety equipment, by boat length

What US federal rules require aboard a recreational boat changes with its length, its engine and where and when you use it. Pick the size closest to yours.

  • A kayak or canoe
  • A boat under 16 feet
  • A boat 16 to 26 feet
  • A boat 26 to 40 feet
  • A boat 40 to 65 feet

For your own boat, exactly: Boat Ready asks its length, engine and waters and gives the list, free.

Check your boat in Boat Ready

This is a reference tool, not an inspection. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to the United States Coast Guard or the Coast Guard Auxiliary. It covers federal carriage requirements only; your state may require more.
