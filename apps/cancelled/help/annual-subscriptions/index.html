Canceled: free guide

How to find yearly subscriptions before they renew

A yearly plan is the easiest subscription to forget and the most expensive to miss. It charges once, at a price large enough to notice, and then says nothing for a year, so by the time it renews nobody remembers agreeing to it.

Why three months of statements cannot find them

A monthly plan shows three times in three months. A yearly plan shows once in twelve, or not at all if it renewed four months ago. The only way to see every yearly plan is a full year of statements, and thirteen months is safer, because a plan that renews on the 3rd can fall just outside a twelve month window.

Where the yearly ones hide

  • Software and apps bought by the year: antivirus, password managers, photo editing, cloud storage
  • Memberships: warehouse clubs, shopping memberships, roadside assistance, professional associations
  • Web domains and website hosting, often set to renew automatically
  • A plan you moved from monthly to yearly for the discount and then forgot about
  • A free trial that turned into a yearly plan rather than a monthly one

Canceled does the reading for you. Open a year of statements and it lists every recurring charge, ranked by what it costs a year, on your own device, with nothing uploaded and no bank login. Seeing the list costs nothing. One payment, never a subscription, shows where to cancel each one.

How to tell a yearly plan from a one-time purchase

One charge on its own proves nothing; you bought something. Two charges from the same company, about a year apart and at the same price, or one step higher, are a yearly plan. With only one year of statements you will often see a single charge from a company that sells subscriptions; treat it as a possible yearly plan and check the account it belongs to.

Watch for a yearly plan paid monthly. Some software sells a yearly commitment billed in twelve monthly payments, and canceling partway can cost a fee. The plan's own account page says which kind you have.

Work out the renewal date

A yearly plan normally renews on the same date as its last charge, one year on. Put that date in your calendar a week early, which gives you time to decide and cancel before it charges. A company can move a billing date, so treat it as an estimate, and check the account page if the date matters.
