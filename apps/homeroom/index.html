What the purchase buys

Pick your state and Homeroom lays out the year's requirements at once, each with the statute it comes from and the date it was last checked: Texas, Florida, North Carolina, California, Ohio, Georgia, Pennsylvania, New York, Michigan and Illinois. Log attendance by the day, with hours where your state counts them, one record per child per day, so a correction never counts twice. Keep a portfolio of work samples by subject and date, each with a photo or a PDF, and a reading log. Tick each requirement as you meet it, put the ones with a month into your own calendar, and download a transcript you issue from the courses you enter.

Good to know

Homeroom sums up state homeschool law for a parent. It is not legal advice, the law changes, and the statute, read with your state's department of education guidance, is the authority; every rule shows its citation and the date Homeroom last checked it. Homeroom counts what you record and cannot tell you whether your district will accept it. Your records and photos stay in the browser you use and are not stored on our server, so download a copy now and then from Account. When a subscription ends, or the school-year pass after June 30, 2027, everything stays readable and downloadable in that browser and new entries pause.
