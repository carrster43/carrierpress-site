ABOUT THIS RECORD

Messages in this app cannot be edited or deleted by either party. Each one
carries a cryptographic hash of the message before it, so removing,
altering or reordering any message breaks every hash that follows it.

This record contains EVERY message between the parties in the period above.
The app provides no way to export a filtered selection.

What this does not establish:
  - It shows that messages were not altered after being sent. It says
    nothing about whether their contents are true.
  - Timestamps are the server's clock. This is not an independent
    timestamping authority.
  - The chain cannot by itself prove that messages after the last one shown
    were not omitted. The totals below are stated so that the other party,
    who holds the same log, can confirm them against their own copy.

What the purchase buys

Start an arrangement with your key, then send the other parent an invite link. They join free, with no key, no account and no email address, and they read and add to the record at no cost. Messages go into a permanent record that neither of you can edit or delete, and each carries a cryptographic hash of the one before it, so a removed or altered message shows. Expenses carry the share asked of the other parent; only the other parent can agree, the amount is fixed once they answer, and only agreed expenses count toward one netted figure of what is outstanding. A calendar says which parent has each child on each day, with a reason when the usual pattern changes. Export the whole record for any period as a text file to keep, print or hand over: every message in the period, never a selection, what it does and does not prove, and the data anyone can check at carrierpress.com/handoff/verify/. Export needs a key of your own.

Good to know

Handoff does not judge who is right, sends no read receipts and gives no legal advice. It is not a substitute for a lawyer or for anything a court has ordered you to use. The record is held on our server, one per arrangement, so both of you see the same one, and nobody can remove the other parent or delete anything in it. Receipt photos are not kept on the web. An arrangement takes up to 300 additions a day. If a subscription ends, both of you can still read and add to the record; export pauses until a key is current again.
