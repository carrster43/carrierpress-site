What the purchase buys

Customers, jobs, quotes and invoices for one person with a van. A job is a title and, if you like, a customer; lines and tax are worked in whole cents, with tax taken once on the taxable lines. Raise the invoice from the job and its amounts are copied, so the invoice you sent stays as sent. Numbers continue your own sequence, and a voided invoice keeps its number. Your payment instructions print on every invoice. Write rough notes and it drafts the scope of work for the customer to sign; when they ask for more, it drafts the change order with the price and the days it adds, or says the request is already in the scope. Ask an electrical code question and the answer cites the NEC article; when it cannot name one, it says so and tells you what to look up.

Good to know

The web version has no card payment link: your customer pays you the way your invoice says, and you mark the money received. Code answers state requirements in our own words and are not the code text; check them against the edition your jurisdiction adopted, state and local amendments, and your inspector. Every draft is checked by you before it goes to a customer. Up to 25 drafts and code answers a day. Your jobs stay in the browser you use and are not stored on our server, and one tab at a time can have Trade Desk open, so download a copy now and then from Account.
