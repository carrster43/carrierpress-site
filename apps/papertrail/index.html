What the purchase buys

Add a receipt, a warranty or an insurance policy as a photo or PDF, or paste a confirmation email, and it reads the vendor, the amount and the date that matters: when cover ends, when a policy renews and charges you, or the last day to return something. Coming up lists them soonest first, saying what happens on each day. Where it could not read a date clearly it asks you to confirm it rather than remind you about a guess. Anything it read but could not place, such as an exclusion, is shown to you, not dropped. The Home section keeps the house's equipment and its recurring jobs beside the documents they came from, puts them in the same list under What runs out next, and makes a service record to hand over when you sell.

Good to know

A browser cannot remind you by itself and there is no email on the web, so Paper Trail puts each date in your own calendar, as an all-day event on the day to act. Your drawer and the scans stay in the browser you use and are not stored on our server, so download a copy now and then from Account; Restore from a copy puts one back. Up to 40 documents can be read a day. It can misread a date: check the document before you act. Bought Founding access? Your key already opens Paper Trail for its first year.
