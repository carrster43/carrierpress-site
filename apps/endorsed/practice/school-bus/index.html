CDL School Bus Endorsement Practice Test

Your state's licensing agency gives the S endorsement knowledge and skills tests, and your state's law sets the rules for stops, lights and loading. Learn your state's CDL manual and school bus laws alongside this.

These are the first 10 of the 30 questions in Endorsed's School Bus (S) Endorsement pack, with the first of its 4 study sections. Every answer names the rule it comes from. The full pack, and 18 other credentials, are in Endorsed.

10 practice questions

  1. What must you hold or qualify for before you can get the S endorsement?

    1. The tank endorsement
    2. The passenger endorsement
    3. A Class A CDL
    Show the answer

    B. The passenger endorsement

    An S applicant must pass the knowledge and skills tests for the passenger endorsement.

    Source: 49 CFR 383.123

  2. Which tests does the S endorsement require?

    1. A knowledge test only
    2. A knowledge test and a skills test
    3. A skills test only
    Show the answer

    B. A knowledge test and a skills test

    School bus, like passenger, takes both a knowledge and a skills test.

    Source: 49 CFR 383.93

  3. What vehicle is the S skills test taken in?

    1. Any bus of the same vehicle group
    2. A school bus of the same vehicle group
    3. A passenger van of any group
    Show the answer

    B. A school bus of the same vehicle group

    The skills test is in a school bus of the same vehicle group as the one you will drive.

    Source: 49 CFR 383.123

  4. Which of these is a topic on the S knowledge test?

    1. External mirror systems and stop signal devices
    2. Air brake adjustment
    3. Cargo securement
    Show the answer

    A. External mirror systems and stop signal devices

    Loading and unloading children, including stop signal devices, mirrors and flashing lights, is a listed topic.

    Source: 49 CFR 383.123

  5. Which is also on the S knowledge test?

    1. Evacuating passengers in an emergency
    2. Fuel tax reporting
    3. Logbook audits
    Show the answer

    A. Evacuating passengers in an emergency

    Emergency exits and procedures for safely evacuating passengers are a listed topic.

    Source: 49 CFR 383.123

  6. You hold a learner's permit with S. May you drive a route with students aboard if your trainer sits behind you?

    1. Yes
    2. No, students may not ride
    3. Yes, on a morning route only
    Show the answer

    B. No, students may not ride

    A CLP holder with S may carry only auditors and inspectors, test examiners, other trainees and the trainer; no students.

    Source: 49 CFR 383.25

  7. Under the CDL rules, which trip counts as school bus use?

    1. Students to a school-sponsored game
    2. A charter company's tour for a senior club
    3. Teachers to a conference on a for-hire coach
    Show the answer

    A. Students to a school-sponsored game

    The CDL definition covers home to school, school to home, and school-sponsored events, but not for-hire carrier operations.

    Source: 49 CFR 383.5

  8. In the safety rules, a school bus is designed or used to carry how many passengers?

    1. More than 8 including the driver
    2. More than 10 in addition to the driver
    3. 16 or more including the driver
    Show the answer

    B. More than 10 in addition to the driver

    More than 10 passengers in addition to the driver.

    Source: 49 CFR 390.5T

  9. School bus operations are excepted from most of parts 390 to 399. Which rule still applies?

    1. The ban on hand-held phones and texting while driving
    2. The 10-hour driving limit
    3. The periodic inspection rule
    Show the answer

    A. The ban on hand-held phones and texting while driving

    The exception keeps 392.80 and 392.82 (texting and phones), 391.15(e) and (f), and 392.15.

    Source: 49 CFR 390.3T

  10. Does federal drug and alcohol testing apply to school bus drivers who need a CDL?

    1. No, school buses are excepted
    2. Yes, the part 390 exceptions do not reach the testing rule
    3. Only for out-of-state trips
    Show the answer

    B. Yes, the part 390 exceptions do not reach the testing rule

    Part 382 covers every CDL driver and says the 390.3T exceptions do not apply to it.

    Source: 49 CFR 382.103

Study: What the S endorsement takes

You must first qualify for the passenger endorsement: pass the P knowledge and skills tests.

Then the S knowledge test covers loading and unloading children, including the stop signal devices, external mirror systems, flashing lights and other safety devices that state or federal law requires on school buses; emergency exits and evacuating passengers; the state and federal laws for railroad crossings; and other operating practices.

The S skills test is taken in a school bus of the same vehicle group as the bus you will drive.

A learner's permit may carry S once you pass the S knowledge test, but the bus may carry no passengers except auditors and inspectors, test examiners, other trainees and the CDL holder training you.

Passenger, school bus and tank vehicle are the only endorsements a learner's permit may carry.

While you train on a permit, the CDL holder supervising you must ride directly behind you or in the first row behind you, watching you drive.

Source: 49 CFR 383.123; 49 CFR 383.93; 49 CFR 383.25
