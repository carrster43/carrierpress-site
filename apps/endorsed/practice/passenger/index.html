CDL Passenger Endorsement Practice Test

Your state's licensing agency gives the P endorsement knowledge test and the skills test in a passenger vehicle. The federal rules below are the same in every state; check your state's CDL manual for anything it adds.

These are the first 10 of the 41 questions in Endorsed's Passenger (P) Endorsement pack, with the first of its 5 study sections. Every answer names the rule it comes from. The full pack, and 18 other credentials, are in Endorsed.

10 practice questions

  1. A van is rated 9,000 pounds and designed to seat 16 people counting the driver. Is it a commercial motor vehicle for CDL purposes?

    1. No, it is under 26,001 pounds
    2. Yes, as Group C, by its design
    3. Only if it carries paying passengers
    Show the answer

    B. Yes, as Group C, by its design

    A vehicle designed to transport 16 or more including the driver falls in Group C even when it is light.

    Source: 49 CFR 383.5

  2. Which tests does the P endorsement require?

    1. A knowledge test only
    2. A knowledge test and a skills test
    3. A skills test only
    Show the answer

    B. A knowledge test and a skills test

    Passenger and school bus endorsements each need a knowledge test and a skills test.

    Source: 49 CFR 383.93

  3. What vehicle is the P skills test taken in?

    1. Any vehicle in the class you hold
    2. A passenger vehicle of the group you will drive
    3. A Group C vehicle, passenger or not
    Show the answer

    B. A passenger vehicle of the group you will drive

    The skills test is taken in a passenger vehicle meeting the requirements of that vehicle group.

    Source: 49 CFR 383.117

  4. You hold a learner's permit with P. Who may ride on the bus while you practise?

    1. Anyone, so long as the trainer is aboard
    2. Only inspectors, examiners, trainees and your trainer
    3. Up to 15 passengers, with the trainer aboard
    Show the answer

    B. Only inspectors, examiners, trainees and your trainer

    A CLP holder with P may carry no passengers other than those listed in the rule.

    Source: 49 CFR 383.25

  5. A CDL shows restriction M. What does it mean for passenger vehicles?

    1. No Class A passenger vehicle
    2. No passenger vehicle of any class
    3. Manual transmission only
    Show the answer

    A. No Class A passenger vehicle

    M means no Class A passenger vehicle; N means no Class A or B passenger vehicle.

    Source: 49 CFR 383.153

  6. After 8 consecutive hours off duty, how many hours may a bus driver drive?

    1. 10
    2. 11
    3. 15
    Show the answer

    A. 10

    Passenger-carrying drivers may drive no more than 10 hours following 8 consecutive hours off duty.

    Source: 49 CFR 395.5

  7. After how many hours on duty may a bus driver no longer drive, following 8 hours off duty?

    1. 14
    2. 15
    3. 16
    Show the answer

    B. 15

    No driving after being on duty 15 hours following 8 consecutive hours off duty.

    Source: 49 CFR 395.5

  8. How long must a bus driver be off duty, consecutively, before the 10 and 15 hour limits start over?

    1. 8 hours
    2. 10 hours
    3. 34 hours
    Show the answer

    A. 8 hours

    The passenger limits run from 8 consecutive hours off duty, not the 10 hours in the truck rule.

    Source: 49 CFR 395.5

  9. Your carrier runs buses every day of the week. What is your weekly on-duty limit?

    1. 60 hours in 7 days
    2. 70 hours in 8 days
    3. 80 hours in 8 days
    Show the answer

    B. 70 hours in 8 days

    70 hours in 8 consecutive days when the carrier operates every day; otherwise 60 in 7.

    Source: 49 CFR 395.5

  10. A motorcoach driver splits sleeper berth time into two periods. What is the shortest either period may be?

    1. 1 hour
    2. 2 hours
    3. 3 hours
    Show the answer

    B. 2 hours

    Neither sleeper period may be shorter than 2 hours.

    Source: 49 CFR 395.1(g)(3)

Study: Who needs the P endorsement

A vehicle designed to transport 16 or more people, counting the driver, is a commercial motor vehicle for CDL purposes even when it is too light for Class A or B. That smaller vehicle is Group C.

To drive a passenger vehicle that needs a CDL you also need the passenger endorsement, shown on the license as P. It takes a knowledge test and a skills test, and the skills test is taken in a passenger vehicle of the group you will drive.

The state can restrict the endorsement by class: restriction M means no Class A passenger vehicle, and restriction N means no Class A or B passenger vehicle.

A learner's permit may carry P once you pass the P knowledge test, but you may carry no passengers except auditors and inspectors, test examiners, other trainees and the CDL holder training you.

The school bus endorsement (S) builds on P: an S applicant must also pass the P knowledge and skills tests. P, S and N (tank vehicle) are the only endorsements a learner's permit may carry.

Passing the tests for a bigger group covers the smaller ones. Group A lets you drive Group B and C vehicles, and Group B lets you drive any Group C vehicle, as long as you hold the endorsements that vehicle needs.

Source: 49 CFR 383.5; 49 CFR 383.93; 49 CFR 383.117; 49 CFR 383.153; 49 CFR 383.25
