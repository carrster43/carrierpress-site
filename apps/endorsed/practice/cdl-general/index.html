10 practice questions

  1. A tractor with a gross combination weight rating of 40,000 pounds pulls a trailer rated 30,000 pounds. Which class is needed?

    1. Class A
    2. Class B
    3. Class C
    Show the answer

    A. Class A

    A combination of 26,001 pounds or more towing a unit rated over 10,000 pounds is Class A.

    Source: 49 CFR 383.91

  2. A straight truck rated 33,000 pounds tows a trailer rated 8,000 pounds. Which class is needed?

    1. Class A
    2. Class B
    3. Class C
    Show the answer

    B. Class B

    A single vehicle of 26,001 pounds or more towing 10,000 pounds or less is Class B.

    Source: 49 CFR 383.91

  3. A van rated 12,000 pounds is built to carry 16 people including the driver. Which class does it need?

    1. None, it is under 26,001 pounds
    2. Class C
    3. Class B
    Show the answer

    B. Class C

    A vehicle designed for 16 or more people counting the driver is a commercial vehicle in Class C when it is not A or B.

    Source: 49 CFR 383.5

  4. How many driver's licenses may a commercial driver hold?

    1. One
    2. One per state where they drive
    3. Two: a CDL and a personal license
    Show the answer

    A. One

    A driver may hold only one license; holding more than one is a violation.

    Source: 49 CFR 383.21

  5. How long must you hold a commercial learner's permit before taking the skills test?

    1. 7 days
    2. 14 days
    3. 30 days
    4. 90 days
    Show the answer

    B. 14 days

    The skills test may not be taken in the first 14 days after the CLP is first issued.

    Source: 49 CFR 383.25

  6. With a CLP in a truck, where must the accompanying CDL holder sit?

    1. Anywhere in the vehicle
    2. In the front seat beside you
    3. In a following vehicle
    Show the answer

    B. In the front seat beside you

    In a truck the CDL holder must be in the front seat beside you; in a passenger vehicle they may sit directly behind or in the first row behind the driver.

    Source: 49 CFR 383.25

  7. A CLP holder has the tank (N) endorsement. What may they drive?

    1. A loaded tank vehicle with a trainer
    2. Only an empty tank vehicle
    3. Any tank vehicle once they have 14 days
    Show the answer

    B. Only an empty tank vehicle

    A CLP holder with N may operate only an empty tank vehicle, purged of residue if it held hazardous materials.

    Source: 49 CFR 383.25

  8. Which endorsements can appear on a commercial learner's permit?

    1. P, S and N only
    2. Any endorsement
    3. H and X only
    Show the answer

    A. P, S and N only

    A CLP may carry only the passenger, school bus and tank endorsements.

    Source: 49 CFR 383.25

  9. The last inspection report lists a defect. What must you do before driving?

    1. Nothing, if the truck seems safe to you
    2. Sign it, once you see the repair certification
    3. Cross out the defect and drive on
    Show the answer

    B. Sign it, once you see the repair certification

    You review the last report and sign it to acknowledge the carrier's certification that the defect was repaired or needed no repair.

    Source: 49 CFR 396.13

  10. You drove a property-carrying truck all day and found no defects. Must you prepare an end-of-day inspection report?

    1. Yes, always
    2. No, not when no defect was found or reported
    3. Only on Fridays
    Show the answer

    B. No, not when no defect was found or reported

    No report is required when no defect or deficiency is found by or reported to the driver.

    Source: 49 CFR 396.11

Study: Who needs a CDL, and which class

Class A covers a combination of vehicles with a gross combination weight rating of 26,001 pounds or more, when the vehicle or vehicles being towed are rated over 10,000 pounds.

Class B covers a single vehicle rated 26,001 pounds or more, or one towing a vehicle rated 10,000 pounds or less.

Class C covers a vehicle or combination that is neither A nor B but is designed to carry 16 or more people counting the driver, or carries hazardous materials in an amount that must be placarded, or any amount of a select agent or toxin.

You may hold only one driver's license at a time. Holding licenses from two states is itself a violation.

A Class A holder may also drive Class B and C vehicles, and a Class B holder may drive Class C vehicles, as long as they hold any endorsement the vehicle needs. Endorsements are needed for double or triple trailers, passenger vehicles, tank vehicles, hazardous materials (a load that must be placarded, or any amount of a select agent or toxin) and school buses. A tank vehicle is one built to haul any liquid or gas in tanks of more than 119 gallons each and 1,000 gallons or more in all.

The skills test vehicle sets restrictions on the license. Test in a vehicle without air brakes and you may not drive one with any type of air brakes; test with air over hydraulic brakes and you may not drive one with full air brakes. Test in an automatic and you may not drive a manual, meaning one with a clutch you work by pedal or lever; any other transmission, semi-automatic included, counts as automatic.

Source: 49 CFR 383.5; 49 CFR 383.91; 49 CFR 383.21; 49 CFR 383.23
