CDL Tanker Endorsement Practice Test

Your state's licensing agency gives the N endorsement knowledge test. The federal rules below are the same in every state; check your state's CDL manual for anything it adds.

10 practice questions

  1. Which of these is a tank vehicle?

    1. One 900-gallon tank
    2. Two 600-gallon tanks
    3. Ten 110-gallon tanks
    Show the answer

    B. Two 600-gallon tanks

    Each tank is over 119 gallons and together they hold 1,000 gallons or more.

    Source: 49 CFR 383.5

  2. What is the aggregate rated capacity that makes a tank vehicle?

    1. 500 gallons or more
    2. 1,000 gallons or more
    3. 3,000 gallons or more
    Show the answer

    B. 1,000 gallons or more

    1,000 gallons or more in total, in tanks each over 119 gallons.

    Source: 49 CFR 383.5

  3. An individual tank must be larger than what capacity to count toward a tank vehicle?

    1. 55 gallons
    2. 119 gallons
    3. 250 gallons
    Show the answer

    B. 119 gallons

    Each tank counts when its rated capacity is more than 119 gallons.

    Source: 49 CFR 383.5

  4. You haul an empty 2,000-gallon storage tank, not built for transport, strapped to a flatbed. Is that a tank vehicle?

    1. Yes
    2. No
    3. Only if it once held fuel
    Show the answer

    B. No

    An empty storage container tank not designed for transportation, temporarily attached to a flatbed, is not a tank vehicle.

    Source: 49 CFR 383.5

  5. Which tests does the N endorsement require?

    1. A knowledge test only
    2. A knowledge and a skills test
    3. A skills test only
    Show the answer

    A. A knowledge test only

    Tank vehicle is a knowledge test only.

    Source: 49 CFR 383.93

  6. What letter shows tank vehicle and hazardous materials endorsements together?

    1. T
    2. X
    3. Z
    Show the answer

    B. X

    X is the code for the combination of tank vehicle and hazardous materials.

    Source: 49 CFR 383.153

  7. A learner's permit holder with N may drive which tank vehicle?

    1. A full tank, with a trainer aboard
    2. Only an empty one, purged if it held hazmat
    3. Any tank under 3,000 gallons, empty or not
    Show the answer

    B. Only an empty one, purged if it held hazmat

    A CLP with N allows only an empty tank, and not one with hazardous materials residue.

    Source: 49 CFR 383.25

  8. You brake hard with a half-full tank. What does the liquid do?

    1. Stays still
    2. Surges forward and pushes the truck ahead
    3. Moves only side to side
    Show the answer

    B. Surges forward and pushes the truck ahead

    Liquid keeps moving when the truck slows, so it runs forward and pushes.

    Source: 49 CFR 383.119

  9. What do baffles control best?

    1. Front-to-back surge
    2. Side-to-side surge
    3. Both equally
    Show the answer

    A. Front-to-back surge

    Baffles slow front-to-back flow; they do little against side-to-side movement.

    Source: 49 CFR 383.119

  10. Which tank surges hardest?

    1. A compartmented tank
    2. A baffled tank
    3. A smooth bore tank with no baffles
    Show the answer

    C. A smooth bore tank with no baffles

    With nothing inside to slow it, liquid in an unbaffled tank surges the most.

    Source: 49 CFR 383.119

Study: What counts as a tank vehicle

A tank vehicle is a commercial motor vehicle designed to carry liquid or gas in a tank or tanks with an individual rated capacity of more than 119 gallons and an aggregate rated capacity of 1,000 gallons or more, attached permanently or temporarily to the vehicle or chassis.

An empty storage tank that is not designed for transportation, rated 1,000 gallons or more and temporarily attached to a flatbed trailer, is not a tank vehicle.

The N endorsement needs a knowledge test only, no skills test. A driver with both N and the hazardous materials endorsement may have them shown together as X.

A learner's permit may carry N once you pass the N knowledge test, but you may drive only an empty tank vehicle, and never one that held hazardous materials unless it has been purged of all residue.

Only three endorsements may go on a commercial learner's permit: passenger (P), school bus (S) and tank vehicle (N).

Source: 49 CFR 383.5; 49 CFR 383.93; 49 CFR 383.153; 49 CFR 383.25
