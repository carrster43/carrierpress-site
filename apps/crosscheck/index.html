Query on account A-55127

November 21, 2026

To the billing office at Riverbend Regional,

I am writing about account A-55127, for services on 2026-07-14.

The bill I received asks me to pay $1,310.00. The Explanation of Benefits from Northgate Health lists my responsibility as $842.00. The two differ by $468.00.

The line items add up to $2,814.00, but the total on the bill is $2,840.00. That is $26.00 apart.

Please could you review the account and let me know how the amount was worked out. If the difference is an error I would be grateful if you could correct it. If it is not, please could you explain what it is for, so I understand what I am being asked to pay.

The letter goes on to offer anything that would help, then your own reason, exactly as you typed it, and your name. Every sentence Crosscheck wrote above is a figure from the two documents, a question or a request.

Free to open

Open Crosscheck, type the figures from the bill and the EOB, and see every finding at no cost. It checks five things: a bill above what the EOB says you owe, bill lines that do not add up to the total, the same line charged twice, EOB figures that do not add up, and service dates that do not match. Each finding says whether it is plain arithmetic or worth asking about.

What the purchase buys

One key drafts the letters for one case, up to three of them: a query to the billing office naming the exact figures, a request for a fully itemised statement, and a letter asking your insurer to look at the claim again. Every sentence Crosscheck writes is a figure from your documents, a question or a request, never a legal claim or a threat. Your own reason goes in exactly as you wrote it. Copy, print or download each letter and send it yourself: Crosscheck sends nothing. Another bill needs another key, and you can choose how many cases at checkout. A Carrier Press Access key on the Apps or All Access tier drafts the letters for any case.

Good to know

Crosscheck compares the figures you type. It does not review coding, so it makes no upcoding, unbundling or medical-necessity judgement, and it does not predict how a billing office or insurer will answer. It is not legal or medical advice. It runs in your browser with no account, and nothing you type is uploaded: your cases stay in that browser, so download a copy now and then from Account. A key is used on a case the first time it opens that case's letters, so open them on the bill you want letters for.
