How to have an EPUB read aloud on a phone or computer

Every phone and computer can read text aloud without anything extra to install. Here is where each one keeps it, and what to do when one flat voice for a whole novel is not enough.

iPhone and iPad

Turn on Speak Screen in Settings, then Accessibility, then Spoken Content. Open the book in your reading app and swipe down from the top of the screen with two fingers. The page is read aloud, and a small panel lets you pause, skip and change the speed.

Mac

Turn on Speak selection in System Settings, then Accessibility, then Spoken Content. Select the text you want and press Option and Escape together. Press them again to stop.

Cast performs a book with a narrator and a voice of its own for every character, right in your browser. The opening of every book on its shelf plays free, with no key and no account.

Android

Turn on Select to Speak in Settings, then Accessibility. Tap its button, then tap or drag over the text on screen to have it read.

Windows

Narrator is built in. Press the Windows key, Control and Enter together to turn it on or off. It is made for reading everything on screen, so it suits a page of text better than a long book.

One voice for every character, or a cast

All four read in one voice. For a novel full of dialogue, Cast reads an EPUB the way a performer would: a narrator, and a separate voice for each character. It reads EPUB files without copy protection, up to 80 MB. Books sold DRM free and books you wrote yourself work; most books bought from the big ebook stores are copy protected, and Kindle files and PDFs are not EPUBs, so Cast cannot open those. Your own books are a membership, with a one-week trial for new members.
