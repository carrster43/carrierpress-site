Cast: free guide

Books performed with a different voice for each character

Every book below is performed in Cast with a narrator and a separate voice for each character. Press the free link under any title to hear how it opens before you decide anything.

How to listen

The free opening plays in your browser on a phone or a computer, with no key and no account. Cast makes its natural voices after one download of about 90 MB; until that finishes, your browser's own voices read. To hear a whole book, buy that book once in Cast; nothing renews.

Cast performs a book with a narrator and a voice of its own for every character, right in your browser. The opening of every book on its shelf plays free, with no key and no account.

Open Cast

62 books on Cast's shelf have a voice for each character. Each title opens its own page; the second link plays its opening in Cast, free.
