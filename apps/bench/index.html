What the purchase buys

The Author desk, run by Jeffrey Carrier of Carrier Press from the craft notes behind his own editions: a listing package (a book description, a subtitle check, seven KDP keywords, three Amazon store categories for KDP and BISAC subjects for IngramSpark, which take different lists), front and back matter (copyright page, title page, About the Author short and long, Also By, the call to your next book), back-cover copy sized to your trim with a tagline and A+ module copy, and pitch emails for a library or an independent bookstore. Every draft is checked by rules you can see: a best-seller or award claim your material does not make, an ISBN or a price you did not type, a placeholder left in, praise that is not word for word, keywords that do not fit KDP. The Errands desk: write down the errand you have been putting off and it plans it in steps (what to find out and where to check it, options side by side, the message ready to send, the form answers to paste) and ends with the one thing you do yourself. A drafted message can only go to an address you typed, and opens unsent in your own mail app. A plan with any step that would act for you is thrown away whole, and you are shown why.

Good to know

Bench drafts and plans. It never sends, posts, files, books or pays for anything, and it does not browse the web, so a plan says where to check things rather than claiming it looked them up, and any web address it writes that you did not give is flagged. What you type goes through our server to Anthropic, which writes the draft, and nothing is stored on our server. Your past work stays in the browser you use, so download a copy now and then. Up to 10 runs a day on each desk. When a subscription ends, past work stays readable in that browser and new runs pause. Drafts are written by an AI model: read every word before you use it.
