Trade Desk privacy policy

Last updated 19 November 2026.

Trade Desk is field service for one person with a van: under a minute of admin per job, usable with gloves on. This policy explains what is kept.

You are storing your customers' details

A job is at somebody's property. Trade Desk holds your customers' names, their contact details and their addresses, and they did not install this app or read this page.

We treat that as your business record. We never contact your customers, never market to them, never build any profile of them, and never sell or share their details with anybody. The only time we reach a customer is when you send them an invoice, because you asked us to.

Money is handled by Stripe, not by us

When a customer pays an invoice, that payment goes through Stripe. Card numbers never reach our servers and we never store them. We keep a record that an invoice was paid and when, which is what a ledger needs.

It works offline, and syncs when it can

Trade Desk is built to work in a basement with no signal, so jobs are recorded on your device first and sent to our servers when a connection returns. Between those two moments the record exists only on your phone.

What is stored

  • Your email address, and whether your subscription is active.
  • Your customers: names, contact details and addresses you enter.
  • Each job, its line items, and what was done.
  • The scope of a job and its change orders, if you use them: your notes, the drafted document, and the name a customer typed to sign one.
  • Invoices, and whether they were paid.

What is not stored

No password, because there is not one. Signing in is by a code sent to your email address. No card numbers, no bank credentials, and no location tracking: a job has an address because you typed one, and your phone is never asked where you are.

Who else is involved

  • Stripe processes payments and holds the card details.
  • Supabase hosts the database and sends the sign in codes.
  • RevenueCat sits between the app and the app store and tracks whether your subscription is active. It receives the purchase events and an identifier for your account.
  • Apple handles the payment itself. Card details go to Apple and never to us.
  • Anthropic drafts a scope or a change order, and only when you tap Draft. It receives that job's title, your customer's name if you entered one, the quoted lines, and the notes or request you typed. It does not receive your customer's address, phone or email, and what it is sent is not used to train anything.

In the phone app, nothing else is sent to a language model.

The web version

Trade Desk in a browser, at carrierpress.com/tradedesk/app/, has no account and no database of ours. Everything above about accounts, syncing, our database host, Stripe, Apple and RevenueCat is about the phone app.

Everything stays in your browser. Your customers, jobs, line items, invoices, scopes, change orders and the names typed to sign them, the payments you mark as received, your business name, default tax rate, payment instructions and invoice count, and your last 20 code questions with their answers are kept in that browser's storage for carrierpress.com, on that device. They are not sent to us and not stored on any server, and nothing is queued to send. Another browser or device cannot see them, so Account offers Download a copy, one file of everything, and Restore from a copy, which reads that file in your browser and, after asking, replaces what is there with it. The file goes wherever you save it; we never see it.

Drafting and code answers. When you tap Draft on a scope or a change order, the job's title, your customer's name if you entered one, the quoted lines, and the notes or request you typed are sent, through a small server of ours on Cloudflare, to Anthropic, and the draft comes back to your browser. When you ask a code question, the question, the NEC edition and the state you chose go the same way. Your customer's address, phone and email are never sent. Neither we nor that server keep any of it, and what is sent is not used to train anything.

No card link. The web version does not use Stripe. The payment instructions you write in Account print on the invoice, and your customer pays you directly.

Your license key. The web version opens only with a license key, from your Gumroad receipt or a Carrier Press Access key. When you paste it on this app's page, it goes to that same server, which asks Gumroad whether the key is good and stores nothing. If it is, your browser keeps the key in an encrypted cookie that scripts on the page cannot read, and about once a day the server asks Gumroad again, so that access ends when a purchase is refunded or a membership ends. The cookie also counts the drafts and code answers asked for that day, so that one key cannot be used without limit. The server keeps no list of keys or visitors. Remove my key from this browser, in Account, deletes it.

Payments for the web version are processed by Gumroad. Trade Desk never receives or stores your payment details. carrierpress.com is served through Cloudflare, which handles your IP address and each request the way any web host does.

To delete it, clear this site's data in your browser's settings. That erases everything Trade Desk kept there, and nobody can bring it back, because we never had a copy. Download a copy first if you need your invoices for your records.

Deleting it

Deleting your account removes your profile, every customer, job, scope, change order and invoice, immediately and unrecoverably. You may need invoices for your own tax records, and this app has no export, so keep whatever you need before you delete: an individual invoice can be shared from the job it belongs to. Stripe keeps its own record of payments, under its terms rather than ours.

On your phone

The app keeps your sign in on your phone, in the phone's secure keychain, so you stay signed in between launches. It also keeps the small rating note described below.

The rating prompt and Contact support

After something in the app has gone well, it may now and then ask Apple to show the App Store's own rating sheet. So that it asks rarely, it keeps one small note on your device: how many times things have gone well, the last two days they did, and the app version it last asked on. Nothing you entered is in that note, and it never leaves the device. Whatever you choose in the rating sheet is handled by Apple.

Contact support opens your own mail app with a message addressed to support@carrierpress.com. The subject line carries the app's name, its version and your phone's system version, so we know which build you mean. Nothing is sent unless you send it, and the app is not told whether you did.
