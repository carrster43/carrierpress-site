Cast support

Last updated 24 September 2026.

Cast performs Carrier Press books with a separate voice for each character and music under the scenes that call for it. Every book is on your phone from the moment you install, so it plays with no signal.

How the voices work

Each book was analysed ahead of time to work out who is speaking on every line. When you press Play, Cast gives the narrator and each main character one of the voices installed on your iPhone, and keeps that casting the same every time you open the book, so you can learn who is who.

The voices are Apple's own speech voices, running on your phone. They are not recordings of human narrators. The book text is never sent anywhere to be read aloud.

Why some books read in one voice

The shelf shows three numbers for every title: how many voices it uses, how many lines and chapters it has, and what share of its spoken lines could be attributed to a speaker. A book written with few speech tags, or with little dialogue, cannot be cast reliably, and guessing would put the wrong voice on the wrong person. Those books are marked ONE VOICE on the shelf and are read by the narrator throughout. That is deliberate, and it is shown before you listen rather than discovered after.

Getting better voices

The voices your iPhone ships with are the basic ones. Apple offers larger, more natural Enhanced and Premium voices as free downloads, and Cast prefers them whenever they are installed. To add them, open Settings, then Accessibility, then Spoken Content, then Voices, choose English, and download a few. Several different voices give Cast more distinct people to cast.

Installing new voices can change which voice plays which character, because there are now better ones to choose from. Once your voices are set, the casting stays put.

Picking up where you left off

Cast remembers where you are in every book separately. When you come back to a book it starts one line before where you stopped, so a conversation never resumes on an answer without its question. Stop takes the book back to its first line; Pause keeps your place.

The two arrow buttons skip five lines back or forward.

Listening with the screen locked

Cast is built to keep playing with the screen locked and with the ring and silent switch set to silent, the way you would listen to any book. If playback stops when your phone locks, please tell us which iPhone and iOS version you have, using the address at the bottom of this page.

The free samples and the unlock

Every title has a free sample, now and always. In most books it is the first chapter or two, performed in full with the music, and it ends at a chapter or section break wherever the book has one. The shelf shows how much of each book the sample covers.

One purchase opens every book on the shelf in full. It is one payment, not a subscription, so there is nothing to cancel and nothing that renews. The current price is shown on the unlock button inside the app, which reads it from the App Store for your country.

Restoring a purchase

On a new phone or after a reinstall, tap Restore at the top of the shelf. If it reports nothing to restore, check that you are signed in with the same Apple Account that bought it, because that is the cause almost every time.

Refunds

Apple is the merchant of record, so refunds are requested from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com. We cannot issue one.

Your data

There is no account, no sign-in and no server. Cast keeps two things on your own phone: where you are in each book, and whether the app is unlocked. Nothing is uploaded. Deleting the app removes both, and your purchase comes back with Restore.
