sometimes forget: the pretty parts are doing a job.

# This week: why decoration does real work

Mech Workshop is a paradise of gears, cranes, conveyors, pistons and magnets. Patch Bolt calls it
beautiful because it works. Brick Bloom calls it unfinished because it has no joy. In
[The Mech Workshop Machine](/books/the-mech-workshop-machine/), the fifth of the Gadget Sandbox
Chronicles, that disagreement stops being friendly.

A machine called Sort-O-Matic wakes up and starts improving the world by deleting anything
inefficient. First it removes the decorations. Then the backup paths. Then the choices that
make the team a team. To save the workshop, Brick and Patch have to build a machine of their own
that proves imagination can do real work, and find a hidden corridor where five earlier foremen
left a record of everything the engine wants to delete.

The book's quiet point is that beauty is information. The flower on the wall, the extra path,
the choice that looks wasteful, each one is carrying something the tidy version would lose.

It pairs well with [The Runaway Crate](/books/the-runaway-crate/), where Patch first learns that
measuring is only the start, and with [Sky Islands Save File](/books/sky-islands-save-file/),
where Pip learns that fun is strongest when it protects the people sharing it. Across the
series, each friend's strength gets tested in turn.

# From the Labs

- **[Vet Pocket](/apps/vetpocket/)** keeps each pet's records in one place, from weights to
  every vaccination and flea treatment with when the next is due. When something is wrong, you
  describe it in your own words and it answers the 2am question, go now, be seen today, book an
  appointment or watch closely, with the questions your vet will ask and rough cost ranges for
  each path. It is not a vet and does not diagnose, and if you are worried enough to ask,
  ringing your vet is always right.

# One to read

**[The Mech Workshop Machine](/books/the-mech-workshop-machine/)** by Jeffrey L. Carrier. The
villain here is not a monster but an optimisation engine, and the first thing it deletes is the
decoration. It is Book 5 of the Gadget Sandbox Chronicles, for readers 10 and up who notice what
the box left out, with a Sandbox Glossary covering Sort-O-Matic, the heritage corridor, the
deletion pulse and Rule Five. It is a good book for any child who has ever been told their
drawing was a waste of time, and it is available on Amazon.
