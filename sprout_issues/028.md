Welcome to Sprout. This week we go to a coffee shop on a barrier island, where the bell above
the door has sounded the same for decades.

# This week: one question, five mysteries

Patchogu Island is six miles of sand and sea grass off the North Carolina Outer Banks. The
Ginger Mae Coffee Shop opens there at seven-fifteen every morning. When the woman the island
called Grandma Knapp dies, she leaves the shop to Victoria, Olivia and Solei, three friends in
their late twenties who only meant to make coffee. She also leaves them a trail, scattered
through cedar boxes, fireproof safes and a back-hallway portrait no one ever looked at twice.

That is the shape of The Marjorie Corey Files. Each book is a mystery that stands on its own,
and each one opens one more drawer in the life of the woman who left the shop behind. In
[The Recipe Notebooks](/books/the-recipe-notebooks/), three of her notebooks turn out to be
written in no recipe anyone could cook: the quantities are in milligrams. In
[The Sea Captain's Portrait](/books/the-sea-captain-s-portrait/), the weathered painting in the
hallway turns out to be an 1847 oil with a history the island never knew it owned.

The cases get bigger as the series goes, but the question underneath never changes. Who was
she, really? The three women answer it together, each bringing what the others lack, and the
series treats them as one complete investigator. It is a cozy with a real secret at its heart,
and a good series to start as the days get long.

# One to read

**[The Morning Table](/books/the-morning-table/)** by Jeffrey L. Carrier. A historian arrives on
Patchogu Island with an anomaly in the records: the woman the island knew as Grandma Knapp does
not appear anywhere before a certain year. What follows is not a chase but a reckoning, with
letters signed in a single set of initials, a name borrowed from a girl who died young, and an
elderly man in a Wilmington nursing home who lost a sister long ago. It is the fifth and final
book of the series, and first-time readers can begin here, though the resolution lands hardest if you
have followed all five. It is available on Amazon.
