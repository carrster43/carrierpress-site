# This week: how a series closes its first arc

The Gadget Sandbox Chronicles give each book its own world inside a building sandbox, and
one rule for building, testing and saving in each. By the sixth book a reader has learned
a whole rulebook alongside Captain Cubemelon's crew, and
[Portal Park](/books/portal-park/) is where that first arc closes.

It is a maze that laughs at maps. One gate leads to the snack path. The snack path leads
back to the first gate. A purple door sends Ember to a blue bridge, and a blue bridge sends
Pip to a sign that says he is already late. It is funny, and then it is not, because the
park starts separating the friends into repeating pieces of the same day.

The rule this book teaches is the hardest one yet: a mystery can wait if your friends
cannot. That is a lot to ask of a hero who loves puzzles, and it is why the finale works.

If your reader is just starting out, begin at [The First Build](/books/the-first-build/):
place the crate, aim the fan, open the portal. What could go wrong? Book five,
[The Mech Workshop Machine](/books/the-mech-workshop-machine/), is the one right before the
finale, and its workshop has forgotten that some flowers are load-bearing. The story does
not stop at six, either. The same crew carries on in The Glitch Blocks, where small
glitches start flickering between the doors.

# From the Labs

- **[Crosscheck](/apps/crosscheck/)** works with two documents: the hospital bill and the
  explanation of benefits your insurer sent for the same care. It looks for itemised
  errors between them and writes an appeal letter that cites its own reasoning.

Every Carrier Labs app is on [the apps page](/apps/).

# One to read

**[Portal Park](/books/portal-park/)** by Jeffrey L. Carrier. Captain Cubemelon loves the
maze until he finds a locked gate labeled ORIGINAL BUILDER, and the harder he tries to open
it, the further the park pulls his team apart. Each friend has to escape a loop built
around their own strength turned into an obstacle, and Moss Guard learns that sometimes
protection is withdrawal. The book ends with a complete Sandbox Glossary covering all six
worlds and the full Rulebook, so a reader who finishes holds the whole first arc in one
place. It is Book 6 of The Gadget Sandbox Chronicles, for ages 10 to 14, and it is
available on Amazon.
