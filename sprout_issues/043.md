# This week: measuring is only the start

The Gadget Sandbox Chronicles follow six friends through six worlds inside a building sandbox, and each book brings one more rule for building, testing and saving. They are written for readers aged 10 to 14 who like to work the puzzle out alongside the characters, and each book includes a Sandbox Glossary of the characters, gadgets and rules.

[The Runaway Crate](/books/the-runaway-crate/) is the second world, Sand Canyon, and it is hot, bright and built to move. Ramps tilt, platforms slide, switches count down, and crates do not wait politely while builders argue. Patch Bolt is sure the route can be fixed with one clean plan. Then a wrong switch sends a crate racing down the rail line and shakes loose a boulder path beneath the canyon, and Patch has to learn that a path has timing, and timing means listening while the build moves.

The series starts with [The First Build](/books/the-first-build/), where Captain Cubemelon's best friend Pip bounces through an unfinished portal and the first test becomes the first rescue. Two books later, [The Frost Cave Reset](/books/the-frost-cave-reset/) sends the team into a cave where every gadget answers seconds late, and Drip Byte has to stop watching his tablet long enough to hear what his teammates see.

Each world adds one rule, and the glossary at the back of each book keeps track of them, so a reader can follow the whole build from the first portal on.

# From the Labs

- **[Fluent Hour](/apps/fluenthour/)** is one spoken conversation a day, about ten minutes, with
  someone patient. Pick a language and a situation, such as a bakery that has run out of what you
  wanted, and the other person speaks first; you answer out loud. There is at most one correction a
  turn, only when a listener would not have understood you, and no praise, streaks or points.

Every Carrier Labs app is on [the apps page](/apps/).

# One to read

**[The Runaway Crate](/books/the-runaway-crate/)** by Jeffrey L. Carrier. The route crate is supposed to
roll, not race, and the moment it races is where this book's rule begins. Book Two of The Gadget
Sandbox Chronicles is a fast building adventure with Dusty Gear, Sandy Switch, Tumblebot,
Pebble Pete and Cactus Jackbot watching every move. It suits readers who measure twice and listen
once, or who need to learn to, and it is available on Amazon.
