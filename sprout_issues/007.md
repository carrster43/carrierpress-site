# This week: the year ahead, one Friday at a time

The year ahead in Sprout looks like this. Every Friday there is one book worth your evening,
pulled from a different corner of the shelves: the restored classics, the mysteries, the
books for young readers, the plain guides to money. Every Friday there is one app from
Carrier Labs, described for what it does and what it will not do. Nothing is sold as more
than it is.

January is when people sit down with their money, so the next few issues lean that way.
The [Money and Income](/money/#h-money-income) shelf is a small set of plain guides to
dividends, side income and automation, written for adults, with no income guarantees.

# From the Labs

- **[Canceled](/apps/cancelled/)** finds every recurring charge in your bank statement, on
  your own device, ranks them by what they cost you in a year, and shows you where to cancel.
  It never connects to your bank, and your statement is read in your browser and never
  uploaded. It is one payment rather than a subscription, which would rather defeat the
  point.

Every Carrier Labs app is on [the apps page](/apps/).

# One to read

**[AI Income Streams](/books/ai-income-streams/)** by Jeffrey L. Carrier. It
is a business book that will not promise passive income in ninety days and does not sell a
course. Instead it lays out one path: pick a specific customer, build a workflow they will
pay for, ship it, and expand from there, with a short Operator Test at the end of every
chapter. It also names the legal lines most business books skip, from hiring rules to the
licensure line for financial advice. Its advice for a first week suits the first week of a
year: a customer you can meet this week, a tool you can start today, a deliverable you can
ship in two. It is available on Amazon.
