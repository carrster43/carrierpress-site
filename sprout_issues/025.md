# This week: a book to read together

A weekend with family is a good time to read with someone rather than beside them.
[Sky Islands Save File](/books/sky-islands-save-file/) is a good one to share. Pip Pop has
found the best world ever: clouds, balloons, fans, floating platforms, and falls that pop
everyone back to the start. At first the resets are hilarious. Then the tools start landing
on the wrong islands, Captain remembers a conversation nobody else had, and Moss Guard
disappears into a save fragment that looks almost, but not quite, like home.

It is the fourth book of The Gadget Sandbox Chronicles. A new reader can start at
[The First Build](/books/the-first-build/), or at book two,
[The Runaway Crate](/books/the-runaway-crate/), where the route crate is supposed to roll,
not race.

  exact boat, answered offline, with the CFR citation and the manual page behind every
  line. It is a reference, not an inspection, and your state may require more.

Every Carrier Labs app is on [the apps page](/apps/).

# One to read

**[Sky Islands Save File](/books/sky-islands-save-file/)** by Jeffrey L. Carrier. The fall
is safe; the save is not. To bring the team back together, Pip has to learn that fun is
strongest when it protects the people sharing it, carry Anchor Block across a fragmented
sky, and make the joke finish. It is a warm, funny book for readers ten and up who love a
joke that lands, available on Amazon.
