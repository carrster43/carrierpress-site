# This week: where every build begins

The Gadget Sandbox Chronicles is a six-book series for ages 10 to 14, and its design is easy to
explain to a child: six friends, six worlds inside a building sandbox, and one more rule for
building, testing and saving in every book. The rules are not handed down. The team earns each
one by getting something wrong first.

In [The First Build](/books/the-first-build/), Captain Cubemelon expects the first test to be
easy: set the spring pad, press the button, save the scene. Instead, every fix uncovers another
rule and every failure teaches something no rulebook ever said. In
[The Runaway Crate](/books/the-runaway-crate/), Patch Bolt is sure one clean plan will fix the
Sand Canyon route, until a wrong switch sends a crate racing down the rail line and he learns
that measuring is only the start, because the path has timing.

By [Portal Park](/books/portal-park/), the first-arc finale, the rules have stacked into
something harder: the idea that a mystery can wait if your friends cannot.

Each book ends with a Sandbox Glossary of the characters, gadgets and rules so far, which is
the sort of thing a keen reader pores over between chapters. The puzzles are there to be solved
alongside the characters, and teamwork is what actually wins.

# From the Press

- **Sand Canyon.** [The Runaway Crate](/books/the-runaway-crate/) is hot, bright and built to
  move, with ramps that tilt, platforms that slide and switches that count down.
- **The first-arc finale.** [Portal Park](/books/portal-park/) is a maze that laughs at maps,
  and it closes on a locked gate labelled ORIGINAL BUILDER.
- **The whole shelf.** [The Gadget Sandbox Chronicles](/young-readers/#h-gadget-sandbox) lists
  all six books in order, starting where every build begins.

# From the Labs

- **[Conception Zodiac](/apps/conceptionzodiac/)** works out the sign overhead at conception
  rather than at birth. It is a pure calculation, with no account and no database, and the
  birthday you enter never leaves your device. It shows the sign, the others it could be and
  the weeks that settle it, and it names a sign, never a date: it is for curiosity, not medical
  advice.

Every Carrier Labs app is on [the apps page](/apps/).

# One to read

**[The First Build](/books/the-first-build/)** by Jeffrey L. Carrier. The very first test in the
Gadget Sandbox becomes the first rescue, when Captain's best friend Pip bounces through an
unfinished portal before the team can lock the build. To bring Pip home, the crew has to turn a
messy lab into a working path before the portal forgets where Pip went. It is Book 1 of the
complete six-book series, with a Sandbox Glossary and the team's first official rule at the
back, and it is a fine way to start a summer of reading. It is available on Amazon.
