# This week: he told you where he got it

In 1926 H. P. Lovecraft wrote a story about a dead professor's papers, a sculptor's dreams
and a city that rose out of the Pacific for one afternoon, and gave modern horror its most
famous name. What is easy to miss is how openly he credited his sources. Arthur Machen is
named in the text. Algernon Blackwood sits at the head of the page. And there is a book on
the shelf that Ambrose Bierce made up.

Every one of those trails leads to a book on the weird fiction shelf. [Carcosa](/books/carcosa/)
shows the first borrowing in order: in 1886 Bierce wrote about a man lost in a ruined
country, trying to find his way back to a city called Carcosa, and in 1895 Robert W.
Chambers took the name, changed what it meant, and built something else on top of it.
[The Great God Pan](/books/the-great-god-pan/) is the Machen of 1894, a doctor's notebook, a
torn manuscript and a witness who stops mid sentence; thirty years later Lovecraft named him
the finest living writer of cosmic fear. And [The Willows](/books/the-willows/) is the
Blackwood, two men on an island in the Danube, which Lovecraft called the finest weird tale
in English without qualification.

Read in that order, the shelf stops being a list of influences and becomes a conversation,
each writer answering the last. Lovecraft's own line sums up the pattern rather well: "What
has risen may sink, and what has sunk may rise."

# One to read

**[The Call of Cthulhu and Other Mythos Tales](/books/the-call-of-cthulhu-and-other-mythos-tales/)**
by H. P. Lovecraft. The title story gave modern horror its most famous name, and this
edition follows every borrowed name in it back to where Lovecraft found it, in forty one
notes on the real books, painters and places and the invented ones. It prints six stories,
from The Picture in the House to The Dunwich Horror, in the order he wrote them and each from
its first magazine printing, so you can watch his invented New England become one world. The
glossary marks plainly which terms Lovecraft never defined and which meanings were added
after his death. It is available on Amazon.
