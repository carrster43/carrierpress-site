# This week: a book to read aloud

Some books are better shared. [The First Flicker](/books/the-first-flicker/), the first of The
Glitch Blocks for ages 8 to 12, is one of them, because its danger is solved by careful
thinking and never becomes a fight.

Drip Byte's map says every route is perfect. It isn't. When a glitch parks itself in a doorway
and a good door starts opening onto the wrong place, the team cannot fight it, fix it or out-run
it. To take a single safe step they have to slow down, test the path, and agree out loud,
together, on the way home. Reading that with someone, a chapter a night, is a gentle way to
practise the same thing.

# From the Labs

- **[Boat Ready](/apps/boatready/)** answers one question about one boat: does it carry what
  federal law requires? Describe the boat and you get the carriage list for that exact boat,
  with the section of the federal rules behind every line. Every result ends by telling you to
  check your state boating agency, because states often require more.

Every Carrier Labs app is on [the apps page](/apps/).

# One to read

**[The First Flicker](/books/the-first-flicker/)** by Jeffrey L. Carrier. One frightened little
glitch already knows Captain's name. This is Book 1 of The Glitch Blocks, a puzzle-forward
adventure about a leader learning that not every urgent signal is the next right step. It is
warm, careful and made for sharing on a long weekend, and it is available on Amazon.
