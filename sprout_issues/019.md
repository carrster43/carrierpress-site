# This week: a cozy with a real secret

Cozy mysteries promise comfort: a small place, a regular table, people who know each
other's orders. The Marjorie Corey Files keep that promise and then hide something real
underneath it.

On Patchogu Island, a six-mile barrier island in North Carolina's Outer Banks, the Ginger
Mae Coffee Shop opens at seven-fifteen every morning, the way it has for forty years. When
the woman the island called Grandma Knapp dies, she leaves the shop to Victoria, Olivia and
Solei, three friends in their late twenties who only meant to make coffee. She also leaves
them a trail, scattered through cedar boxes, fireproof safes and a back-hallway portrait
nobody ever looked at twice.

Book by book, one quiet death pulls a thread that runs far past the island. It begins with
a photograph from Dallas in [The Second Shooter](/books/the-second-shooter/), carries on
through [The Recipe Notebooks](/books/the-recipe-notebooks/) and
[The Sea Captain's Portrait](/books/the-sea-captain-s-portrait/), and ends with the
borrowed name of the woman at the center of it all.

What makes it work as a series is the investigator, who is really three people: three
women who together make one complete detective. Each case stands on its own. Together they
answer the question the first page asks, which is who she was, really.

  in your browser, using the computer voices your browser provides. Its page plays a few

  hear how it sounds before you buy a book.

# One to read

**[The Marjorie Corey Files: The Complete Series](/books/the-marjorie-corey-files-the-complete-series/)**
by Jeffrey L. Carrier. Behind one quiet death on a small island sit a sixty-year-old
conspiracy, a suppressed compound worth a fortune, a forged Civil War land deed and a
town's deleted history. All five Patchogu Island mysteries are here in one volume, warmly
atmospheric and quietly literary, built around three women and the grandmother whose
secrets they spend five books learning to read. Start it on a long spring weekend and you
will not want to stop between cases. It is available on Amazon.
