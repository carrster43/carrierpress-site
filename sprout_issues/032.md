# This week: a cold book for a hot week

Frost Cave is the third world in the Gadget Sandbox Chronicles, and it is beautiful, blue and
dangerous in a quiet way. Every step slides too far. Every gadget answers seconds late. Every
ice bridge remembers old pressure. Nothing in it is out to get anyone. It simply does not
respond the way you expect, and the delay is the puzzle.

[The Frost Cave Reset](/books/the-frost-cave-reset/) gives the lead to Drip Byte, who trusts
his tablet more than anyone on the team. When the cave's reset button is frozen and his scan
starts showing what the cave used to be instead of what it is, the numbers stop being enough.
Drip has to look up from the screen and listen to what his teammates see, hear and feel.

The series moves through very different weather. The next world,
[Sky Islands Save File](/books/sky-islands-save-file/), is clouds, balloons, fans and falls
that pop everyone back to the start. After that, [The Mech Workshop Machine](/books/the-mech-workshop-machine/)
is all gears, cranes and pistons. Each world tests a different friend, and each book adds one
rule to the team's rulebook.

For readers aged 10 to 14 who like their puzzles patient, a book about cold that rewards slow
thinking is a good companion for a long, warm afternoon indoors.

# From the Labs

- **[Downpour](/apps/downpour/)** makes rain for tabletop game masters: on the tavern roof,
  over a camp in the forest, rolling in as the boss fight starts. Every drop is made live in
  your browser, so there is no recording and no loop for the table to notice. Layer up to three
  of its nine surfaces, pick a ready scene, or let a Night Arc such as Storm passing build to a
  peak and ease off over ninety minutes.

# One to read

**[The Frost Cave Reset](/books/the-frost-cave-reset/)** by Jeffrey L. Carrier. The team has
only one careful run to save the portal core, in a cave where the truth arrives in pieces and
always a little late. It is Book 3 of the Gadget Sandbox Chronicles, for readers 10 and up, with
a Sandbox Glossary covering the cave's characters, the six-beat hum, the Crystal Key and Rule
Three. It is a story about listening to the people beside you as well as the numbers, and it is
available on Amazon.
