Welcome back. This week is about the youngest readers on the shelves, and the words they
need before any of the others.

# This week: words for what is going on inside

There is a shelf on the site called Money Skills and Making Things. It holds books about
side hustles, startups, investing and building things online, all written for readers who
are starting early. And sitting among them is a coloring book about feelings.

It belongs there. Before a child can plan a lemonade stand or stick with a project that is
not working yet, they need a name for frustrated, and bored, and brave. Most grown-ups
remember being told to calm down. Fewer remember being asked what the feeling was called.

[All About Feelings](/books/all-about-feelings/) does the asking, one page at a time. There
are fourteen feelings in it, from happy and sad to jealous, lonely, silly and grateful, and
each page has a prompt to fill in about when that feeling turns up. It is written for ages
5 to 8, and it ends with a note to the grown-ups.

If you have a child at the table this weekend, grateful is a good page to start on. It sits
in the book right beside jealous and bored, which is about how it goes in real life too.

The rest of the shelf picks up where that leaves off. [Safe Online Projects for
Kids](/books/safe-online-projects-for-kids/), for ages 8 to 14, teaches staying safe online
by building things, from a coded maze game to a published webpage, rather than by listing
scary rules.

# From the Labs

- **[Through the Gate](/apps/throughthegate/)** shows the record a resume parser actually
  keeps from your resume and, more to the point, everything it threw away. Paste the text in
  and you see the fields it kept, the job titles it renamed with your own wording beside them,
  and every piece that did not survive, each with the plain reason it was lost. It reads the
  way a competent general parser does, so it names no employer's system and does not predict
  whether you will be screened in or out.

Every Carrier Labs app is on [the apps page](/apps/).

# One to read

**[All About Feelings](/books/all-about-feelings/)** by Jeffrey L. Carrier. The book's last
lesson is three short lines: all feelings are okay, all feelings can be talked about, and
every child deserves a safe place to understand what is happening inside. It is a coloring
book for ages 5 to 8, with large pages for crayons, markers and colored pencils, a Feelings
Reflection Wheel, and prompts that turn coloring into conversation. It works at home, in a
classroom or at quiet time, and it is a gentle way to start a habit of talking. It is
available on Amazon.
