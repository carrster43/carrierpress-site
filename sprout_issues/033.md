# This week: hearing what noise hides

A loop in Portal Park will not stop repeating the same broken phrase, over and over, going
nowhere. The whole team is sure it is a trap. Pip Pop is not so sure, and so instead of fighting
it, Pip does the bravest thing there is: stops, and listens.

That is the heart of [The Loop That Spoke](/books/the-loop-that-spoke/), the third of The Glitch
Blocks for ages 8 to 12. Reversed and re-heard, the loop turns out to carry a message that has
been trying to find its way home for a very long time. The thing in the way is not an enemy. It
is a friend who lost the words. In [The Between Badge](/books/the-between-badge/), at the end of
the series, that long-lost message is finally made whole.

# From the Labs

- **[Through the Gate](/apps/throughthegate/)** shows the record a resume parser builds from
  your resume: the fields it kept, the job titles it renamed with your own wording beside them,
  and every piece that did not survive, each with the plain reason it was lost. It is not any
  one employer's system, and it does not predict whether you will be screened in or out.

Every Carrier Labs app is on [the apps page](/apps/).

# One to read

**[The Loop That Spoke](/books/the-loop-that-spoke/)** by Jeffrey L. Carrier. The help comes from
a bouncy little ring named Loopi and a signpost that talks backwards. It is a warm, surprising
adventure about patience and kindness, for any child who has been told to ignore the noise. A
good one for a holiday weekend, and it is available on Amazon.
