# This week: the book for the quiet hour

Every Christmas Day has a lull, usually somewhere after lunch, when the house goes still and
someone finds a chair by the window. That hour wants a particular kind of book: warm, a
little funny, and about someone deciding to be happy.

[The Blue Castle](/books/the-blue-castle/) is that book. L. M. Montgomery wrote it for
adults in 1926 and set it far from Prince Edward Island, in the pine and water country of
Ontario's Muskoka. Readers who grew up on Anne of Green Gables often find it later, and keep
passing it on.

# One to read

**[The Blue Castle](/books/the-blue-castle/)** by L. M. Montgomery. At twenty-nine, Valancy
Stirling has never been loved, never been free, and never once said what she thinks, and
then one letter changes the rules. A short life lived fully, she decides, is worth more than
a long one spent obeying. This edition has the complete 1926 novel, notes on the real
Muskoka places, and a reader's guide for book clubs. It is a good gift for anyone who needs
permission to start, and it is available on Amazon.
