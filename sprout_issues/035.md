# This week: every friend gets a turn

A team story can easily become one hero and a few helpers. The Glitch Blocks, six books for ages
8 to 12, avoids that by handing the hard lesson to a different member of Captain Cubemelon's crew
in each book.

In [The First Flicker](/books/the-first-flicker/), it is Captain, learning that not every urgent
signal is the next right step. In [Static Orchard](/books/static-orchard/), it is Brick Bloom,
the mark-maker, who always believed a sign drawn once would hold forever. Pip Pop takes
[The Loop That Spoke](/books/the-loop-that-spoke/) by listening, and Patch Bolt takes
[The Broken Save](/books/the-broken-save/) by keeping his hands still.

[The Moving Line](/books/the-moving-line/) belongs to Moss Guard, who has spent his whole life
learning to stand still and hold the line. Then the team has to cross a bridge that splits as
they step: one lane racing ahead, one dragging behind, one frozen solid. Run, and it drops you.
Freeze, and it drifts away. Moss has to learn that a shield can guard a path while the path
moves, and Ember Slice sets the beat so the whole team can move as one.

A child reading the series is likely to find a favourite, and to see that each one's strength
turns, at some point, into the thing they have to learn around.

# From the Press

- **Brick's book.** [Static Orchard](/books/static-orchard/) is about memory, friendship and a
  tiny tree-sprout named Sprig Signal, in an orchard that keeps saving itself wrong.
- **The finale.** [The Between Badge](/books/the-between-badge/) puts the whole team on one
  impossible bridge, each holding a piece no one else can.
- **Not sure where to start?** [Find your next book](/pick/) asks who the book is for and what
  kind of story they like, then gives you one to begin with.

# From the Labs

- **[The Binder](/apps/thebinder/)** is the if-something-happens file: where the will is, which
  accounts exist, who to ring and what you want. Nine short sections take under an hour and a
  half in all, and everything is encrypted in your browser with a passphrase only you know.
  Nothing is ever released to anyone automatically, so you download a printable copy to keep
  with the will and tell one person you trust where it is.

Every Carrier Labs app is on [the apps page](/apps/).

# One to read

**[The Moving Line](/books/the-moving-line/)** by Jeffrey L. Carrier. The only way across is a
bridge that will not hold still, and no one can cross it alone. It is Book 5 of The Glitch Blocks,
a thrilling, big-hearted adventure about rhythm, trust and moving together, and by its end the
seventh badge, pale for so long, finally begins to warm. It is worth reading for the moment a
protector learns to move, and it is available on Amazon.
