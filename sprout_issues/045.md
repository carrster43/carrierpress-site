# This week: what a town cuts from its record

A small-town newspaper is a strange kind of memory. It records every storm, every election and every shop opening, and in doing so it becomes the place a town goes to check what happened. Which means that whoever controls the archive controls the past.

The Marjorie Corey Files are built on that. Three women co-inherit the Ginger Mae Coffee Shop on Patchogu Island, off the North Carolina Outer Banks, and with it sixty years of what the town agreed not to say out loud. Each book stands alone and opens one more drawer in the life of the woman who left the shop behind.

In [The Prattler Files](/books/the-prattler-files/), the longtime editor of the Patchogu Prattler dies and leaves the paper's archive to the island historical society. Victoria, Olivia and Solei volunteer to help digitize it. The Prattler has kept a continuous account of the island since 1934, except for one gap: four months of 1969 and 1970 have been cut clean from the binding. On those pages were a land dispute that split the island's two churches, a fire at the north end, and a young woman who was last seen at the Ginger Mae and never seen again.

The series begins with [The Second Shooter](/books/the-second-shooter/), a JFK assassination thread and a secret the island kept for sixty years. If you would rather have the whole shelf in one volume, there is [The Marjorie Corey Files: The Complete Series](/books/the-marjorie-corey-files-the-complete-series/).

# One to read

**[The Prattler Files](/books/the-prattler-files/)** by Jeffrey L. Carrier. Ruth Helen Bright, the young
woman who vanished from the missing pages, is alive. She is seventy-seven, and she has been waiting fifty
years for someone to ask. Book 4 of The Marjorie Corey Files is a warmly atmospheric island mystery
about the record a town keeps and the harm it chooses to repair, with an estate, a nephew who wants
certain files left buried, and a grandmother who spent three years protecting people. Settle in with it
as autumn starts; it is available on Amazon.
