Titrate privacy policy

Last updated 15 November 2026.

Titrate handles what a GLP-1 prescription does not: side effects logged against dose changes, protein targets sized to your weight, and a written record to take to the next appointment. It will not tell you whether a symptom is normal. That is a judgement for the person who prescribed the medication, and the app is built to refuse it rather than approximate it. This policy explains what is kept.

This is health information, and it is treated that way

Your doses, your symptoms and your weight are information about your body and about a medication you are taking. There is no useful way to soften that, so the rest of this page is specific rather than reassuring.

It never recommends a dose

Worth stating on the privacy page because it limits what the app is for. Titrate records the dose your prescriber set and what happened at it. It does not suggest increasing, decreasing or skipping anything, and nothing in it is medical advice. Dose decisions belong to you and your prescriber.

Weight is stored, and that is worth a sentence

Weight is one of the more sensitive things a person tracks, and people using a GLP-1 have often had a long relationship with being weighed. It is kept because change over time is the point of the record. It is never shown to anyone else, never compared to anybody, and never used to advertise anything to you.

What is stored

  • Your email address, and a subscription status field that is unused in this version.
  • Each dose: what was taken and when.
  • Each symptom log: what you felt, how strongly, and on what day.
  • Weights you record, with their dates.

What is not stored

No password, because there is not one. Signing in is by a code sent to your email address. No prescriber's details, no pharmacy, no prescription number, no insurance information and no payment card details.

Who can see it

You, and nobody else using the app. Every table checks the signed in account against the owner of the row before returning anything. There is no clinician view, no telehealth integration and no sharing feature. Nothing is sent to a language model, we do not sell any of it, and no employer or insurer has any route to it.

Who else is involved

Supabase hosts the database and sends the sign in codes. This app has no in-app purchase in this version, so nothing about a payment or a subscription reaches Apple or anyone else.

The web version

Titrate in a browser has no account and no database. Everything above about accounts, sign in codes and our database is about the phone app.

Your record stays in your browser. The doses you record, the symptoms you log with any notes, your weights and your choice of pounds or kilograms are kept in that browser's storage for carrierpress.com, on that device. They are not sent to us and not stored on any server. Another browser or device cannot see them, and clearing the site's data in your browser erases them, so the You tab offers Download a copy of my record. Restore from a copy reads a downloaded copy in your browser and adds what is not already there; it never replaces anything, and the file is not sent anywhere.

Your license key. The key check is the only request the web version makes, and it carries nothing you have logged. The web version opens only with a license key, from your Gumroad receipt or a Carrier Press Access key. When you paste it on this app's page, it goes to a small server of ours on Cloudflare, which asks Gumroad whether the key is good and stores nothing. If it is, your browser keeps the key in an encrypted cookie that scripts on the page cannot read, and about once a day the server asks Gumroad again, so that access ends when a purchase is refunded or a subscription ends. The server keeps no list of keys or visitors. Remove my key from this browser, on the app's page, deletes it.

Payments are processed by Gumroad. Titrate never receives or stores your payment details. carrierpress.com is served through Cloudflare, which handles your IP address and each request the way any web host does.

To delete it, use the You tab, then Erase my record from this browser. Nothing about your record is held anywhere else to delete. Lock Titrate in this browser forgets your key here and keeps the record for when you enter it again.

Deleting it

Deleting your account removes your profile and every dose, symptom log and weight, immediately and unrecoverably.

On your phone

The app keeps your sign in on your phone, in the phone's secure keychain, so you stay signed in between launches. It also keeps the small rating note described below.

The rating prompt and Contact support

After something in the app has gone well, it may now and then ask Apple to show the App Store's own rating sheet. So that it asks rarely, it keeps one small note on your device: how many times things have gone well, the last two days they did, and the app version it last asked on. Nothing you entered is in that note, and it never leaves the device. Whatever you choose in the rating sheet is handled by Apple.

Contact support opens your own mail app with a message addressed to support@carrierpress.com. The subject line carries the app's name, its version and your phone's system version, so we know which build you mean. Nothing is sent unless you send it, and the app is not told whether you did.
