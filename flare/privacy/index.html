Flare privacy policy

Last updated 16 November 2026.

Flare is a symptom diary for chronic conditions and for perimenopause, with a Doses section for anyone on a GLP-1 medicine. You record how you felt on a given day, and Flare looks for things that occur together. This policy explains what is kept, where it sits, and who can reach it.

This is health information, and it is treated that way

What you type into Flare is information about your body. There is no useful way to soften that, so the rest of this page is specific rather than reassuring.

Finding patterns, or building an appointment script, sends your diary to Anthropic

This is the part you are least likely to assume, so it is here rather than at the bottom. Flare does not analyse anything until you ask it to. When you tap to find patterns on the Insights screen, or ask for what to say at an appointment, up to your 180 most recent days are sent to Anthropic to be read by a language model, and that includes the symptom scores, the hours you slept, the stress rating, the foods you listed and anything you wrote in the notes field. An appointment script also sends the cycle pattern you chose, if you track perimenopause.

Nothing is sent unless you ask for an analysis or a script, and nothing is sent for an analysis until at least seven days are logged. Anthropic processes the request on our behalf and does not use it to train models. If you would rather none of your diary ever left the database, do not use the pattern finding feature; every other part of Flare works without it.

What is stored

  • Your email address, and which condition you chose when you signed up.
  • One record per day you fill in: the severity you gave each symptom on a nought to ten scale, hours slept, a stress rating, any foods you listed, and anything you wrote in the notes field.
  • If you track perimenopause, the cycle pattern you describe.
  • If you track doses, the medicine, each dose and the day it started, your weight, and the changes you mark on a day.
  • The patterns Flare generates from those days, together with the stretch of dates each one was drawn from, and the appointment scripts it builds for you.
  • Whether your subscription is active, and when the current period ends.

What is not stored

No password, because there is not one. Signing in is by a code sent to your email address. No name, no date of birth, no address, no phone number, no insurance or payment details. The app has nowhere to put them.

Who can see it

You, and nobody else using the app. Every table checks the signed in account against the owner of the row before it returns anything, so another Flare user cannot reach your days even if they go looking. There is no sharing feature, no clinician view, and no household or family account.

  • Supabase hosts the database and sends the sign in codes.
  • Apple processes the subscription payment. Apple tells us whether a subscription is active. It never receives anything you recorded.
  • RevenueCat records that same active or inactive state so the app knows what to unlock. It receives no symptoms, no notes and no diary.
  • Anthropic receives your diary entries when, and only when, you ask Flare to find patterns or to build an appointment script, as described above.

That is the complete list. Flare contains no analytics, no advertising and no tracking software of any kind. Nothing is sold, nothing is shared with anybody else, and none of it is used to train anything.

Flare is not medical advice

A pattern that two things happened near each other is not a finding about your health, and Flare does not diagnose, treat or rule anything out. The doctor summary exists to make an appointment easier to prepare for, not to replace one. If something is getting worse, speak to a clinician.

The web version

Flare in a browser has no account and no database. Everything above about accounts and our database is about the phone app.

Your health log stays in your browser. Entries, the condition you selected, your cycle pattern if you track perimenopause, the doses, weights and changes you record, and your pattern reports and appointment scripts are kept in that browser's storage, on that device. They are not sent to us and not stored on any server. Clearing the site's data in your browser erases them, so Account offers Download a copy of my log.

Pattern analysis and appointment scripts. When you ask for patterns, or for what to say at an appointment, your browser sends the entries (and, for a script, the cycle pattern you chose) to our server (a Cloudflare Worker), which passes them to the Anthropic API and returns the result to your browser. Neither our server nor Cloudflare keeps them. Anthropic does not use data submitted through its API to train its models.

Your license key. The web version opens only with a license key, from your Gumroad receipt or a Carrier Press Access key. When you paste it on this app's page, it goes to a small server of ours on Cloudflare, which asks Gumroad whether the key is good and stores nothing. If it is, your browser keeps the key in an encrypted cookie that scripts on the page cannot read, and about once a day the server asks Gumroad again, so that access ends when a purchase is refunded or a subscription ends. The server keeps no list of keys or visitors. Remove my key from this browser, on the app's page, deletes it.

Payments are processed by Gumroad. Flare never receives or stores your payment details. carrierpress.com is served through Cloudflare, which handles your IP address and each request the way any web host does.

To delete it, use Account, then Erase my log from this browser. It is immediate and cannot be undone, by you or by us, because no other copy exists. Lock Flare in this browser forgets the key but keeps the log.

Deleting your account

From the Account tab, at any time, without asking us. It is immediate and permanent: your sign in, your profile, every day you recorded and every pattern generated from them are deleted together. Nothing is retained in a backup copy for later use, and there is no restore. If you want your diary, export the doctor summary before you delete.

Cancelling a subscription is separate and is handled in your Apple subscription settings, because Apple owns that relationship rather than us.

On your phone

The app keeps your sign in on your phone, in the phone's secure keychain, so you stay signed in between launches. It also keeps the small rating note described below.

The rating prompt and Contact support

After something in the app has gone well, it may now and then ask Apple to show the App Store's own rating sheet. So that it asks rarely, it keeps one small note on your device: how many times things have gone well, the last two days they did, and the app version it last asked on. Nothing you entered is in that note, and it never leaves the device. Whatever you choose in the rating sheet is handled by Apple.

Contact support opens your own mail app with a message addressed to support@carrierpress.com. The subject line carries the app's name, its version and your phone's system version, so we know which build you mean. Nothing is sent unless you send it, and the app is not told whether you did.
