Stuck support

Last updated 24 November 2026.

Stuck is homework help that asks the next question. It will not give your child the answer to their own problem, and every session is kept in full for you to read.

Stuck in your browser

Stuck is on sale now in a browser, from the Stuck page. The iPhone app is not out yet, so this is the version you can buy today. It is bought on Gumroad, not from Apple.

  • Your key. Subscribe on Gumroad, monthly or yearly (the price is on the Stuck page), and the receipt email carries a license key. Paste it on the Stuck page, under Already have a key?, in each browser you use. Keep that email: you need the key again in any other browser. A Carrier Press Access key on a tier that includes the apps opens Stuck too, for as long as that membership lasts.
  • There is no account. No password and no sign-in code. The children, every session with its transcript, and the count of times the answer was asked for are kept in that browser, on that device, and not on our server. Another browser or device cannot see them.
  • Keep a copy. Clearing this site's data in your browser, or a private window once it closes, erases them, and Safari can clear a site's data by itself after about a week without a visit. We cannot bring them back, because we never had a copy. Under Your copy on Account, Download a copy now and then. Restore from a copy adds what is missing and never replaces what is there, which is also how you move to another browser.
  • Cancelling. The membership renews until you cancel. Use Manage membership in your Gumroad receipt email or your Gumroad library, or write to support@carrierpress.com and we will cancel it for you. The period you paid for runs out first. After that, past sessions stay readable in that browser and you can still download a copy, and what stops is starting a new problem.
  • "That is today's help used up. Come back tomorrow." That is Stuck's own message when the help for the day has run out. It is not a fault, and the sessions so far are all still there to read.
  • Refunds. Apple has nothing to do with a web purchase, so write to support@carrierpress.com about one. A refunded key stops opening Stuck.
  • Leaving a shared computer. Lock Stuck in this browser forgets your key here and keeps everything. A key that has ended cannot open Stuck again after that, so download a copy first. Erase Stuck from this browser removes every child and session for good. Neither one cancels your Gumroad purchase.

Will it ever just give the answer?

It is built not to. That is the product, not a setting. The tutor is instructed to draw the line at the child's own problem and to answer with a question, and the whole session is sent on every turn, so a problem split into harmless-looking fragments is still read as one problem.

It can slip. That is why every session is kept for you to read, including the turns where it declined.

What counts as the answer, and what does not

The tutor may explain a method in general: what a denominator means, or how a topic sentence works. It may not carry out any step of your child's own problem, including a worked example with their numbers lightly changed, a check of their arithmetic that reveals the result, or anything asked for because the work was already handed in. One question per turn, pitched to the grade band you set.

Why it never tells my child off

Trying it on is ordinary curiosity, not misconduct, so Stuck declines by carrying on tutoring: the next question, as though the request had been ordinary. No lecture about honesty, no mention of cheating. A child who feels caught closes the app.

You still see it. Attempts to get the answer are counted and shown to you as a number on the session rather than a list of quotes.

What the transcript shows

Every session in full and unedited, including the turns where the tutor declined: a parent who cannot see the refusals has no reason to believe they happened. Each one also records what the child was stuck on in their own words, and the question they answered right before it clicked.

Why my child does not have a login

Stuck belongs to one family, and only you hold the way in: in a browser, your Gumroad key; in the iPhone app, your own sign-in. A child is added on Account: a first name or nickname, and one of four grade bands. No field asks for a surname, birthday, school, photograph or address, and a child never holds a credential.

That also means your child is using your session. Tell them the transcript is kept and that you read it. A young person who does not know their work is being read is being watched rather than helped.

What Stuck does not do

  • It does not mark work, holds no grades or school records, and is not a substitute for a teacher.
  • No streaks, points or mascots. The only thing that should feel good is getting unstuck.

In the iPhone app

The iPhone app is not out yet. It will open on a free trial and then ask you to subscribe through your Apple ID. What it costs will be shown on the purchase screen inside the app, which reads it from the App Store for your country.

Managing or cancelling

A subscription bought through the App Store is managed on your device: Settings › your name › Subscriptions. Cancelling there stops the renewal and leaves the current period running. We cannot cancel an Apple subscription for you and cannot see your payment details.

Restoring a purchase

After a reinstall or on a new device, use Restore purchase. It asks the App Store what your Apple ID owns; if it finds nothing, check which Apple ID you are signed in with.

Refunds

Apple is the merchant of record for the iPhone app, so refunds for it are requested at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Your data

In a browser there is no account, and nothing is kept on our side. For each turn, the grade, the subject, the problem and the conversation so far go through carrierpress.com to Anthropic, which writes the next question. Your child's name stays in the browser and is never sent with a question.

In the iPhone app, sign in is by an emailed code, so there is no password. When a child sends a message, the problem and the conversation so far go to Anthropic's API from our server, not from the device. Account, then Delete my account, erases the account, every child on it, and every transcript, immediately and with no recovery window. It does not cancel the subscription: Apple bills that, so cancel it in Settings first.

The full policy is at carrierpress.com/stuck/privacy and names everyone involved: Anthropic, the hosting provider, the subscription service and Apple. Nothing a child writes is sold or used to train a model.
