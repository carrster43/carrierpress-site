Paper Trail privacy policy

Last updated 17 November 2026.

Paper Trail turns the pile of paperwork into facts with dates, and warns you before something lapses. This policy explains what is kept.

Everything you photograph is sent to Anthropic to be read

This is the part you are least likely to assume, so it is here rather than at the bottom. The whole point of the app is reading documents, and that reading is done by a large language model run by Anthropic. Every image you add is sent in full.

Because the app takes whatever paperwork you have, that can be a wider range of information than you intended in the moment: an insurance policy carries a policy number, a receipt can carry the last four digits of a card, a warranty carries your address. Photograph the document you want tracked, and cover anything on it you would rather not send.

What is stored

  • Your email address, and whether your subscription is active.
  • Every document image you add, kept so you can find the original again.
  • The structured record read out of each one: what it is, what it covers, and the dates that matter.
  • Alerts for expiry, and a record of reminders sent so the same one is not sent twice.
  • Your home, if you keep it here. The Home section is optional and starts only when you add something to it. It holds what you tell it or what a document you file says: the equipment in the house (its kind, make, model, serial, the year it was fitted, its filter size, its warranty date), the maintenance jobs scheduled for it, the jobs you mark done, and the work read off invoices and inspection reports (who did it, their phone number if printed, the date and the cost). An address, only if you type one in for the top of the service record.

The service record you hand over. When you choose to download or share it, it is made on your device from the Home section and goes only where you send it. It names contractors and costs, so read it before you send it.

What is not stored

No password, because there is not one. Signing in is by a code sent to your email address. Nothing is asked for as a field beyond what you choose to photograph, and no payment card details ever reach us.

Who can see it

You, and nobody else using the app. Every table checks the signed in account against the owner of the row before returning anything. There is no household or shared view. We do not sell any of it, we do not send it to advertisers, and we do not analyse your documents for anything except the dates you asked us to track.

Who else is involved

  • Anthropic receives every document you photograph.
  • Supabase hosts the database and sends sign in codes.
  • Resend delivers reminder emails, and receives your email address and the contents of that reminder.
  • RevenueCat sits between the app and the app store and tracks whether your subscription is active. It receives the purchase events and an identifier for your account.
  • Apple handles the payment itself. Card details go to Apple and never to us.

The web version

Paper Trail in a browser has no account and no database. Everything above about accounts, sign in codes, Supabase, Resend, RevenueCat and stored images is about the phone app.

Your drawer stays in your browser. The records read from your documents, the scans themselves (each photo or PDF, kept beside the dates read from it), and everything in the Home section (your equipment, its jobs, its history and any address you typed) are kept in that browser's storage for carrierpress.com, on that device. They are not sent to us and not stored on any server. Another browser or device cannot see them, and clearing the site's data in your browser erases them, so Account offers Download a copy of my drawer, scans and the Home section included. Restore from a copy reads a downloaded copy in your browser and adds what is not already there; it never replaces anything, and the file is not sent anywhere.

Documents you choose to have read. A photo or PDF you pick, or an email you paste, is sent, through a small server of ours on Cloudflare, to Anthropic to be read, and the reading comes back to your browser. The whole document goes, including anything on it you were not thinking about, such as an address or an account number. Neither we nor that server keep the document or the reading. Photos are shrunk in your browser before they leave.

Reminders. The web version sends no reminders and no email. When you choose Add to my calendar, your browser makes a calendar file with the item's title, the vendor, the date and how it was read (for a house job, its name, the equipment and the due date), and saves it to your device for you to open in your own calendar. It is not sent to us.

Your license key. The web version opens only with a license key, from your Gumroad receipt or a Carrier Press Access key. When you paste it on this app's page, it goes to that same server, which asks Gumroad whether the key is good and stores nothing. If it is, your browser keeps the key in an encrypted cookie that scripts on the page cannot read, and about once a day the server asks Gumroad again, so that access ends when a purchase is refunded or a membership ends. The cookie also counts the documents read that day, so that one key cannot be used to read without limit. The server keeps no list of keys or visitors. Remove my key from this browser, on the app's page, deletes it.

Payments are processed by Gumroad. Paper Trail never receives or stores your payment details. carrierpress.com is served through Cloudflare, which handles your IP address and each request the way any web host does.

To delete it, open Account, then Erase my drawer from this browser. Nothing about your drawer is held anywhere else to delete. Lock Paper Trail in this browser forgets your key here and keeps the drawer for when you enter it again.

Deleting it

Deleting your account removes your profile, every document image, every record and every alert, immediately and unrecoverably. If a document is the only copy you have, save it elsewhere before you delete.

On your phone

The app keeps your sign in on your phone, in the phone's secure keychain, so you stay signed in between launches. It also keeps the small rating note described below.

The rating prompt and Contact support

After something in the app has gone well, it may now and then ask Apple to show the App Store's own rating sheet. So that it asks rarely, it keeps one small note on your device: how many times things have gone well, the last two days they did, and the app version it last asked on. Nothing you entered is in that note, and it never leaves the device. Whatever you choose in the rating sheet is handled by Apple.

Contact support opens your own mail app with a message addressed to support@carrierpress.com. The subject line carries the app's name, its version and your phone's system version, so we know which build you mean. Nothing is sent unless you send it, and the app is not told whether you did.
