Cleared support

Last updated 1 November 2026.

Cleared is a study app for the CDL and the transport credentials around it. It has 19 packs and 828 practice questions, and every answer names the federal rule, FEMA course or state law it rests on. It opens in a browser at carrierpress.com/cleared/app. The iPhone app is not out yet.

What Cleared is not

Cleared is study preparation. It is not the exam and it does not issue anything. Your state licensing agency gives the CDL knowledge and skills tests; the FAA, the Coast Guard, the FRA, TSA and FEMA set the tests for their credentials. Where a figure comes from a state manual rather than a federal rule, the app marks it and tells you to check your own state's manual, because that is what you will be tested on.

What is on the shelf

  • Road: CDL General Knowledge, Air Brakes, Combination Vehicles, HazMat, Passenger, School Bus, Tank Vehicle, Doubles and Triples, TWIC, Federal Fleet Driving, Limousine and Chauffeur, Medical Specimen and Supply Courier, Animal Transport, and Armored Car and Cash in Transit.
  • Air: FAA Part 107 Remote Pilot and the Private Pilot Knowledge Test.
  • Water: USCG Captain (OUPV).
  • Rail: Locomotive Engineer and Conductor.
  • Emergency response: FEMA NIMS and ICS core courses.

Every pack was checked item by item against its cited text by a reviewer who did not write it before it went on the shelf. The CDL packs also have a pre-trip examiner: you say each item out loud, as you would to an examiner, and tick it off.

What is free

The first study section and the first 10 practice questions of every pack, and the engine compartment part of the pre-trip examiner, are free in your browser. No account and no email are needed to use them.

Subscribing on the web

One subscription, monthly or yearly, opens every pack, including packs added later. On the web it is sold through Gumroad; the current price is on the Cleared page. After you subscribe:

  • Your license key is on the Gumroad receipt email.
  • Open Cleared, tap Unlock every pack, and paste the whole key.
  • Do this once in each browser you study in. There is no account, so the key is how a browser knows you have subscribed.

If the key reads as not recognised, copy it again from the receipt and paste all of it. If it still fails, write to us with the email address you used at Gumroad.

A Carrier Press Access key works too, on the Apps or All Access tier. The Books tier covers the books only and does not open Cleared.

How the subscription is checked

Cleared asks Gumroad about your key about once a day. If you are offline, the packs stay open for up to 7 days after the last time Gumroad said yes.

Cancelling

Cancel from your Gumroad library (gumroad.com/library). Cancelling does not close the packs straight away: you keep them for the days you have already paid for, until Gumroad marks the subscription as ended.

Why my packs closed

The packs close in a browser when Gumroad says the subscription has ended, a renewal payment did not go through, or the payment was refunded or disputed. Your progress is not touched. Renew from your Gumroad library, then open the unlock screen and tap Check again.

The unlock screen also has Remove the key from this browser. That closes the packs in that browser only. It does not cancel the subscription, and pasting the key again opens them.

Your progress

Progress is kept in the browser you study in, not on a server, so it does not follow you to another browser or device. Clearing that browser's site data, or studying in a private window, removes it. The details are on the privacy page.

The study record

Subscribers can print a record of every pack they have practised, or send it as a link. The record travels inside the link itself; nothing is stored on our side, and anyone you send it to can see what is on it. It is self-reported practice. It is not a license, a test result or a training certificate, and it says so on its face.

The jobs board

The Jobs screen lists job-search links, ten of them government sites, and a members' board for help-wanted ads and graduates offering their services. Members post by email to jobs@carrierpress.com in the format the app fills in for you. Each ad is checked before it goes up, and ads that look like scams, discriminate or offer illegal work are turned down.

Reporting an error in a question

Every answer shows the rule it rests on, for example 49 CFR 383.91(a). If you think an answer is wrong, write to us with the pack, the question and, if you can, the rule or manual page you are reading. Rules change: when a regulation is amended the affected questions are checked again, and corrections go out in the next update to the web app.
